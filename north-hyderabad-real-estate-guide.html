Why Consider North Hyderabad

North Hyderabad is the value story of the city. Areas like Kompally and Medchal offer greenery, gated communities and noticeably lower prices than the western IT core, making them ideal for buyers who prioritise space and affordability, or who commute toward Secunderabad, the airport and the NH-44 corridor. On the north-west edge, Bachupally and Miyapur blend north-Hyderabad value with proximity to the western IT belt.
