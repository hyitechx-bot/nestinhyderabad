A Landmark High-Rise Gated Community in Kukatpally

Closer to life, closer to each other. Kukatpally and the KPHB belt remain one of Hyderabad's most established and best-connected residential markets — mature social infrastructure, metro on the doorstep and direct ORR access to the western IT corridor. This landmark development brings large-format, high-rise living to that address: a 20-acre gated community with 75% open space and a 4.3-acre forest-themed central park.

Planned across 13 residential towers rising up to 25 floors, the community is anchored by one of Hyderabad's largest clubhouses — a 1,36,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse. It is developed by a well-established Hyderabad builder and carries both Telangana RERA registration (P02200011363) and GHMC approval — giving buyers scale, lifestyle and legal confidence in one address.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of 3 BHK flats here?
Pre-launch pricing starts from around ₹1.2 Cr for a 3 BHK, with early-bird advantages for the first set of bookings. Final pricing depends on floor, facing and tower.

What configurations are available?
Spacious 3 BHK homes across three size bands — 1,565 / 1,575 sq.ft, 1,800 / 1,830 / 1,880 / 1,905 sq.ft and 2,150 / 2,205 sq.ft — with 2 balconies, 2–3 toilets and a separate utility.

Is it RERA and GHMC approved?
Yes — Telangana RERA P02200011363 and GHMC approved.

How far is it from the Metro, ORR and airport?
Three Metro stations within 2 km, direct ORR access, and under 30 minutes to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport.

How big is the community?
20 acres with 75% open space, 13 towers rising up to 25 floors, a 4.3-acre forest-themed central park and a 1,36,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse.

Is there an indoor swimming pool, padel court and pickleball court in this Kukatpally community?
Yes. The amenities include an indoor temperature-controlled swimming pool, an outdoor swimming pool, a VRV golf course, a pickleball court, a padel court, a mini scooter court, a boxing ring, a kids' adventure zone and a butterfly garden, set around a 4.3-acre forest-themed central park.

Are there 3 BHK flats of about 1,800 sq ft or 2,000 sq ft, and which have 3 toilets?
The 1,800, 1,830, 1,880 and 1,905 sq.ft bands suit a 3 BHK of about 1,800 sq ft. There is no exact 2,000 sq.ft plan; the nearest are 1,905 and 2,150 sq.ft. The larger size bands have 3 toilets.

Which metro stations and malls are near this 20-acre gated community?
Kukatpally Metro, Kukatpally Y Junction and Bharat Nagar metro stations are each about 2 km away. Manjeera Mall, Forum Sujana Mall, Ashoka One and LakeShore are about 2.6 km away, and the project has direct ORR access.
