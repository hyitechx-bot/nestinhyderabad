About the Project

Where Luxury Meets the Lake

Experience elevated urban living in an iconic high-rise gated community in Kondapur, designed for families who value spacious homes, premium amenities and beautiful lake views.

Premium Lake View High-Rise in Kondapur

This iconic high-rise gated community spans 9.11 acres in the heart of Kondapur, with 7 towers rising to 47 floors. It offers premium 2.5, 3 & 3.5 BHK apartments from 1,571 to 2,415 sq.ft — many with beautiful Gopi Cheruvu lake views and a generous 11 ft ceiling height.

Residents enjoy a 1,00,000 sq.ft grand clubhouse and extensive lifestyle amenities, with convenient connectivity toward Gachibowli, the Financial District and HITEC City. Priced at ₹8,699/sq.ft with a home loan option available.
