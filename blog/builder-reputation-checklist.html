Builder Reputation Checklist – 7 Things to Research Before You Invest
Trust, But Verify Everything

Your dream flat is only as good as the builder behind it. Here's exactly how to verify any builder's credibility before handing over your hard-earned money.

📝 Buyer Education•July 30, 2026•5 min read

1. Check Their RERA Registration History

Visit the Telangana RERA website (rera.telangana.gov.in) and search for the builder. Look at how many projects they've registered, whether any registrations were revoked, and if there are complaints filed against them. A builder with multiple RERA-registered projects and zero complaints is a strong green flag.

2. Read Google Reviews & Social Media

Search for "[Builder Name] reviews" on Google. Look specifically for:

  • Reviews from actual residents (not sales-time reviews)
  • Complaints about construction quality or delays
  • How the builder responds to negative feedback
  • Facebook groups and Twitter mentions for unfiltered opinions

3. Visit Their Completed Projects

This is the most powerful check. Visit a project they delivered 3-5 years ago. Look at wall quality, common area maintenance, waterproofing condition, and overall build quality. New projects always look good — the real test is how they age.

4. Talk to Existing Residents

Spend 30 minutes in a completed project's common area. Talk to residents about:

  • Was possession given on time?
  • Were promises kept (amenities, specifications)?
  • How responsive is the builder to post-possession issues?
  • Any hidden charges that appeared later?

5. Check Delivery Timeline History

Compare the builder's promised delivery dates (from old brochures or RERA filings) with actual possession dates. A pattern of 1-2 year delays is a serious red flag. Consistent on-time delivery across multiple projects shows operational strength.

6. Search for Legal Cases

Check the Telangana High Court and district court websites for any cases against the builder. Look for consumer forum complaints too. Multiple legal disputes, especially related to possession delays or fraud, should make you very cautious.

7. Assess Financial Stability

A financially stressed builder is your biggest risk. Look for signs:

  • Are they offering unusually high discounts or desperate payment plans?
  • Do they have too many ongoing projects relative to their size?
  • Check if their older projects are fully sold or still have unsold inventory
  • Look for MCA filings if they're a registered company

The Bottom Line

Spending 2-3 days on this research can save you from years of legal battles and financial stress. Never rely solely on a builder's marketing — verify everything independently. A reputable builder with a clean track record is worth paying a small premium for.
