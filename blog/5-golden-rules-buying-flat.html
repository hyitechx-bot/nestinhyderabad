5 Golden Rules Before Buying Any Flat
Remember These Forever!

Five powerful, non-negotiable rules that protect every flat buyer. Pin these to your wall, save them on your phone — and never compromise on any of them.

📝 Buyer Education•July 30, 2026•5 min read

Rule #1: Never Buy Without RERA Registration

This is rule number one for a reason. RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority) registration means the project has been legally vetted, approvals are in place, and the builder is accountable to a government body.

Why it matters:

  • Your money goes into an escrow account — builder can't divert it to other projects
  • Possession date is legally binding — delays attract compensation
  • All project details (approvals, layout, carpet areas) are publicly verifiable
  • You have a legal forum for complaints if things go wrong

No RERA = No consideration. Period. Verify at rera.telangana.gov.in before even visiting the site.

Rule #2: Always Compare on Carpet Area, Not Super Built-Up

Super built-up area (SBU) includes common areas like lobbies, staircases, and sometimes even amenity spaces. Two flats with the same "1,800 sqft SBU" can have vastly different actual living spaces — one might give you 1,250 sqft carpet while another gives only 1,100 sqft.

The formula: Always calculate price per carpet sqft. If Flat A is ₹7,000/sqft on SBU but has 70% carpet ratio, your effective cost is ₹10,000/sqft on carpet. Compare that with Flat B honestly.

Rule #3: Budget 15-20% Above the Base Price

The "starting at ₹75 Lakhs" you see in ads is just the beginning. Here's what gets added:

  • GST: 5% (on under-construction properties)
  • Registration & Stamp Duty: ~7.5% in Telangana
  • Parking: ₹3-8 Lakhs (often not included in base price)
  • Corpus/Maintenance Deposit: ₹1-3 Lakhs
  • Interiors & Moving: ₹5-15 Lakhs (basic to good quality)

If your budget is ₹1 Crore, look at flats priced ₹80-85 Lakhs base. This keeps you financially comfortable with no last-minute funding stress.

Rule #4: Visit the Location During Rush Hour

Every location looks peaceful on a Sunday morning. The real test is Monday morning at 9 AM and evening at 7 PM. Visit during peak hours to check:

  • Actual commute time to your office (not Google Maps estimate)
  • Traffic congestion at key junctions near the project
  • Road conditions — are they actually built or just "proposed"?
  • Noise levels from nearby roads, construction, or commercial areas

A 15-minute difference in daily commute = 10 hours wasted every month. Over 10 years, that's 1,200 hours of your life sitting in traffic.

Rule #5: Get Independent Legal Verification

Never rely on the builder's lawyer to verify the builder's documents. That's like asking a student to grade their own exam. Hire an independent property lawyer (costs ₹15,000-25,000) to verify:

  • Land title and ownership chain
  • All government approvals (HMDA/DTCP, environmental clearance)
  • Encumbrance certificate (no loans or disputes on the land)
  • Agreement of sale terms and hidden clauses

This ₹20,000 investment can save you from a ₹1 Crore mistake. It's the cheapest insurance you'll ever buy.

Remember These Forever

These five rules aren't suggestions — they're shields that protect your biggest financial investment. Builders who object to any of these checks aren't worth your trust or your money. A good builder welcomes scrutiny because they have nothing to hide.
