Why the Master Plan Matters Before You Pick a Home

Most buyers compare projects on price and amenities, then choose a unit almost at random. In a four-tower community that is a mistake. Your tower decides which sizes you can choose from, which way your living room faces, how far you are from the clubhouses and how much of the community's green space you look onto.

Candeur Eternia in Bachupally, just off the Miyapur Extension and about 5 minutes from the ORR Mallampet exit, is planned as four towers with two clubhouses and a long list of outdoor zones. This guide uses the published tower plan to show what each tower offers, so you can shortlist the right one before a site visit. For the full project overview, see the Candeur Eternia project page.

How the Master Plan Is Organised

The master plan numbers about 40 features across the campus. Rather than listing all of them, here is how they group together:

  • Arrival zone: entrance, security cabin, children's waiting area and a stepped plaza at the front of the site.
  • Two clubhouses: Clubhouse 1 is dedicated to fitness, sports and wellness; Clubhouse 2 handles dining, events and guest stays. They sit at opposite ends of the campus, so tower position changes your walking distance.
  • Active outdoors: two pickleball courts, a cricket net, half basketball court, mini soccer court, cycling track, trim trail, outdoor gym and a playzone for children.
  • Social and quiet spaces: party lawn, great lawn, amphitheatre, people's plaza, seating plazas, reading deck, yoga lawn, hammock garden and a senior citizen pavilion.
  • Everyday extras: a pet park, sensory walk, gazebo, viewing deck and a swimming pool with deck.

Which Tower Suits Which Buyer?

Looking for a 2 BHK? Start with Tower D

Tower D is the only tower with the smallest homes: 1,462 sq.ft (east-facing) and 1,546 sq.ft (north-facing). It is also the only tower with north-facing units. If you want the most affordable entry point, or a compact home for a couple or a small family, this is where to look first.

Want a 2.5 BHK (study or home-office room)? Look at Towers B and C

The 2.5 BHK sizes are about 1,590 sq.ft in Tower C (west-facing, units 8 to 11) and 1,619 sq.ft in Tower B (east-facing, units 8 to 12). Both give you a bedroom-sized extra room that works well for remote work, a nursery or a guest room. Choose Tower B if you prefer morning light and Tower C if you prefer a west-facing home.

Need a family-sized 3 BHK? You have the widest choice

  • 1,874 sq.ft is offered in all four towers, in both east and west facings. It is the most flexible size if you want to choose by tower position.
  • 2,004 sq.ft is offered in Towers A, B and C.
  • 2,192 sq.ft, the largest home, is available in all four towers.
  • 1,711 sq.ft (Tower B east) and 1,777 sq.ft (Tower C west) sit between the 2.5 BHK and the 1,874 sq.ft options, if you want a slightly more compact 3 BHK.

East, West or North: How to Think About Facing

Facing is partly practical and partly personal. A few honest points:

  • East-facing homes get morning sun, which many families prefer for living areas and the master bedroom. Many buyers also choose east for Vastu reasons.
  • West-facing homes get strong afternoon sun. In a Hyderabad summer that can mean warmer rooms, but it also means bright evening light and good cross-ventilation if the layout is open. Ask to see the balcony orientation on site.
  • North-facing homes are limited to Tower D (1,546 sq.ft, units 4 to 6) and tend to be softer and cooler.

Visit at the time of day you will actually be home. A site visit at 11 a.m. tells you little about a west-facing living room at 5 p.m.

Amenities Across the Two Clubhouses

Clubhouse 1: fitness, sports and social living. Two swimming pools, a cardio gym, aerobics lounge, two badminton courts, a squash court, table tennis and indoor games, pool room, yoga and meditation room, preview theatre, senior citizen lounge, crèche, conference room, society office, spa, two doctor's consulting rooms, an outdoor cafeteria, a supermarket and a pharmacy.

Clubhouse 2: hospitality and celebrations. A restaurant and dining hall, banquet hall, multipurpose hall, two premium guest suites and ten guest rooms.

Together these add up to 68,000+ sq.ft of amenities. More detail is on the amenities page.

Specifications and What Is Not Included

  • Structure: RCC monolithic shear-wall slab structure with a 2.985 m floor-to-floor height on residential floors.
  • Flooring and walls: vitrified tiles of reputed make; premium acrylic paint over two coats of putty.
  • Windows and doors: UPVC windows and balcony sliders with provision for fly mesh; engineered-wood main door with PU polish.
  • Lifts: automatic passenger lifts in every tower plus a service lift, with ARD, surveillance and intercom.
  • Power and safety: 100% DG backup with auto changeover, EV charging provision in the basements, fire hydrant and sprinkler system, CCTV and a fire command centre.
  • Water: hydro-pneumatic supply, a sewage treatment plant with treated water reused for irrigation and flushing, and rainwater collection.

A point many buyers miss: the specifications state that no kitchen counters are provided; only plumbing for a sink is given, along with electrical and plumbing provisions for an RO, chimney and exhaust. Budget for your own modular kitchen.

Approvals: RERA and HMDA

Candeur Eternia is registered with Telangana RERA under P02200010709 and has HMDA building permit 001399/HMDA/00375/SWBP/MDL1/2025. To check it yourself, open rera.telangana.gov.in, go to Registered Projects and search the registration number. Compare the sanctioned plan, timeline and unit areas there with what is in your agreement.

8 Questions to Ask Before You Choose a Tower

  1. What is the exact RERA carpet area of the unit I am choosing?
  2. Which floors are available in this tower, and is there a floor-rise charge?
  3. What will my balcony and living room look onto in 3 to 4 years?
  4. How far is this tower from Clubhouse 1 and Clubhouse 2?
  5. Where are the lifts, the service lift and the parking entry relative to my flat?
  6. What is the maintenance charge per sq.ft and what does it cover?
  7. What is the current construction stage and the RERA possession date?
  8. What is the all-in cost, including GST, registration and parking?
