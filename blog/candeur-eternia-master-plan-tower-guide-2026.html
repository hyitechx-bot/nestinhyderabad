Why the Master Plan Matters Before You Pick a Home

Most buyers compare projects on price and amenities, then choose a unit almost at random. In a four-tower community that is a mistake. Your choice of home decides your size, how far you are from the clubhouses and how much of the community's green space you look onto.

Candeur Eternia in Bachupally, just off the Miyapur Extension and about 5 minutes from ORR Exit 4, is planned as four towers with two clubhouses and a long list of outdoor zones. This guide sets out the confirmed unit sizes by BHK type, so you can shortlist the right home before a site visit. For the full project overview, see the Candeur Eternia project page.

How the Master Plan Is Organised

The master plan numbers about 40 features across the campus. Rather than listing all of them, here is how they group together:

  • Arrival zone: entrance, security cabin, children's waiting area and a stepped plaza at the front of the site.
  • Two clubhouses: Clubhouse 1 is dedicated to fitness, sports and wellness; Clubhouse 2 handles dining, events and guest stays. They sit in different parts of the campus, so tower position changes your walking distance.
  • Active outdoors: two pickleball courts, a cricket net, half basketball court, sub soccer, cycling track, trim trail, outdoor gym and a playzone for children.
  • Social and quiet spaces: party lawn, great lawn, amphitheatre, people's plaza, seating plazas, reading deck, yoga lawn, hammock garden and a senior citizen pavilion.
  • Everyday extras: a pet park, sensory walk, gazebo, viewing deck and a swimming pool with deck.

Which Size Suits Which Buyer?

Looking for a 2 BHK?

The 2 BHK homes are 1,462 sq.ft and 1,546 sq.ft. If you want the most affordable entry point, or a compact home for a couple or a small family, start here.

Want a 2.5 BHK (study or home-office room)?

The 2.5 BHK sizes are 1,590 sq.ft and 1,619 sq.ft. Both give you a bedroom-sized extra room that works well for remote work, a nursery or a guest room.

Need a family-sized 3 BHK?

  • 1,711 sq.ft is the most compact 3 BHK, just above the 2.5 BHK sizes.
  • 1,874 sq.ft and 2,004 sq.ft are the mid-range family sizes.
  • 2,192 sq.ft is the largest home in the project.

Choosing a Facing

Facing is partly practical and partly personal. A few honest points:

  • East-facing homes get morning sun, which many families prefer for living areas and the master bedroom. Many buyers also choose east for Vastu reasons.
  • West-facing homes get strong afternoon sun. In a Hyderabad summer that can mean warmer rooms, but it also means bright evening light. Ask to see the balcony orientation on site.

Ask our team which facings are currently available for your preferred size. Visit at the time of day you will actually be home. A site visit at 11 a.m. tells you little about a west-facing living room at 5 p.m.

Amenities Across the Two Clubhouses

Two swimming pools, cafeteria, cardio gym, gym, aerobics lounge, badminton courts, a squash court, table tennis and indoor games, pool room, yoga room, preview theatre, senior citizen room, crèche, conference room, spa, two doctor rooms, a super market and a pharmacy.

Specifications and What Is Not Included

  • Structure: RCC monolithic shear-wall slab structure.
  • Flooring and walls: vitrified tiles of reputed make; premium emulsion paint over two coats of putty.
  • Windows and doors: UPVC windows and balcony sliders with provision for fly mesh; African teak main door with teak veneer flush shutter, PU polished.
  • Lifts: automatic passenger lifts in every tower plus a service lift, with ARD, surveillance and VF drive technology.
  • Power and safety: 100% DG backup with auto changeover, provision for common EV charging stations in Basement-1 (DG backup excludes EV charging), fire hydrant and sprinkler system, CCTV and a fire command centre.
  • Water: hydro-pneumatic supply, a sewage treatment plant with treated water reused for irrigation and flushing, and rainwater collection.

A point many buyers miss: the specifications state that no kitchen counters are provided; only plumbing for a sink is given, along with electrical and plumbing provisions for an RO, chimney and exhaust. Budget for your own kitchen counters and cabinets.

8 Questions to Ask Before You Choose a Tower

  1. What is the exact RERA carpet area of the unit I am choosing?
  2. Which floors are available in this tower, and is there a floor-rise charge?
  3. What will my balcony and living room look onto in 3 to 4 years?
  4. How far is this tower from Clubhouse 1 and Clubhouse 2?
  5. Where are the lifts, the service lift and the parking entry relative to my flat?
  6. What is the maintenance charge per sq.ft and what does it cover?
  7. What is the current construction stage and the RERA possession date?
  8. What is the all-in cost, including GST, registration and parking?
