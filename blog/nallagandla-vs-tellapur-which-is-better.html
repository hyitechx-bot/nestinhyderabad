Nallagandla vs Tellapur 2026
Which Area Is Better to Buy a Flat?

Established convenience versus emerging space — two neighbouring west-Hyderabad areas compared on price, connectivity, infrastructure and investment potential.

📊 Area Comparison • September 2026 • 8 min read

The Quick Verdict

Choose Nallagandla if you want an established neighbourhood with mature social infrastructure and convenient access to the western IT belt and Lingampally. Choose Tellapur if you want newer, larger gated communities with more open space and better value, and you're comfortable being a little further out. The two sit close together, so it's really maturity vs newness.

Price & Positioning

Factor Nallagandla Tellapur
Positioning Established, convenient Emerging, spacious
Relative price Modest premium Better value
Social infra Mature Developing fast

Connectivity & Commute

Nallagandla is well-connected to the western IT belt and Lingampally, with established road links that make daily commutes convenient. Tellapur relies on the ORR and improving arterial roads; it's very comfortable for car commutes and gets better each year as infrastructure fills in.

Lifestyle & Infrastructure

Nallagandla offers a settled, ready-to-live environment with popular communities and existing amenities. Tellapur is where many of the newest large-format gated communities are coming up, offering more open space and modern amenity design for buyers who want fresh product.
