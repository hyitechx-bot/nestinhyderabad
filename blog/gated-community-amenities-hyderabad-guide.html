Gated Community Amenities in Hyderabad
What to Look For in 2026

Pools, courts, guest rooms and parks add real value to daily life, but only if they are actually built and maintained. Here is a practical checklist, with real examples from two RERA approved projects. Examples: Candeur Eternia, Bachupally and Candeur Kukatpally (Candeur Arbora) (RERA P02200011363).

📘 Buyer Guide • Updated September 2026 • 8 min read

Why Amenities Matter More Than You Think

In a gated community you pay for amenities through the price and through monthly maintenance. A long list in a brochure is not enough. What matters is how many residents share each facility, whether it is built in the first phase, and whether it suits your family's routine.

The Checklist

  • Swimming pool: is there more than one, and is there an indoor option?
  • Gym and fitness: cardio and strength areas, plus an aerobics or yoga space.
  • Racquet and ball courts: squash, badminton, pickleball, basketball or a cricket net.
  • Kids and seniors: a crèche, play zones, a senior citizen room and a walking track.
  • Guest rooms and hospitality: useful for visiting relatives, so you do not need a larger flat.
  • Daily needs: a pharmacy, a super market and doctor rooms inside the community.
  • Open space and greenery: parks, a cycling track, a pet park and tree-lined paths.
  • Utilities: power backup, EV charging provision, water treatment and fire safety.

Example 1: A 7.7-Acre Community in Bachupally

This RERA approved project (P02200010709) has two clubhouses and a 40-item outdoor master plan.

  • Clubhouse 1 (68,000+ sq.ft): two swimming pools, cardio gym and gym, spa, squash court, badminton courts, preview theatre, table tennis and indoor games, aerobics lounge, yoga room, crèche, senior citizen room, two doctor rooms, pharmacy and a super market.
  • Clubhouse 2: restaurant / dining hall, banquet hall, multi-purpose hall, 12 guest rooms and an open terrace.
  • Outdoors: a 40-item master plan with two pickleball courts, cricket net, cycling track, trim trail, amphitheatre, great lawn, pet park, outdoor gym, half basketball court and play zones.

The 100% DG backup (excluding EV charging), EV charging provision in Basement-1, garbage chute in every tower, and a sewage treatment plant with rainwater collection are part of the specifications.

Example 2: A 20-Acre Community in Kukatpally

This RERA approved (P02200011363) and GHMC approved project offers 3 BHK homes only, and focuses on scale and open space.

  • Clubhouse: a 1,36,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse.
  • Sports and fitness: an indoor temperature-controlled pool, an outdoor pool, a VRV golf course, pickleball, padel, a mini scooter court and a boxing ring.
  • Kids and nature: a kids' adventure zone, a butterfly garden and a 4.3-acre forest-themed central park.
  • Layout: 20 acres, 13 towers up to 25 floors and 75% open space.

Questions to Ask the Seller

  1. Which amenities are in the first phase of construction?
  2. What is the monthly maintenance, and what does it cover?
  3. Are paid-use facilities, like guest rooms or the spa, charged separately?
  4. Who maintains the pools and gyms after handover?
  5. What is the RERA registration number?

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Hyderabad gated communities have two swimming pools?

The 7.7-acre Bachupally project in this guide has two swimming pools in Clubhouse 1. The 20-acre Kukatpally project has an indoor temperature-controlled pool and an outdoor pool.

Do any projects have guest rooms?

Yes. The Bachupally project has 12 guest rooms in Clubhouse 2, along with a restaurant, banquet hall and multi-purpose hall.

Is there a pickleball court?

Yes. The Bachupally project has two pickleball courts and the Kukatpally project also has pickleball.

What is the open space in the Kukatpally project?

The Kukatpally project has 75% open space across 20 acres, including a 4.3-acre forest-themed central park.

Are amenities included in the price?

Amenities are part of the project, but maintenance is paid monthly and some paid-use facilities may be charged separately. Ask for the full cost and maintenance sheet.
