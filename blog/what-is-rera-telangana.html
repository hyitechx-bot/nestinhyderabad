What is RERA? How It Protects Home Buyers in Telangana
Your Complete Guide for 2026

RERA changed the real estate game in India. Here's everything you need to know about how it protects your money, your timeline, and your rights as a buyer in Telangana.

📝 Buyer Education•July 30, 2026•5 min read

What is RERA?

RERA stands for the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016. It's a central government law that requires all real estate projects to be registered with a state-level regulatory authority before they can be advertised or sold.

In Telangana, the regulatory body is Telangana RERA (TS-RERA), and all projects in Hyderabad, Rangareddy, Medchal-Malkajgiri, and other districts must comply.

Which projects need RERA registration?

  • Land area exceeding 500 square meters, OR
  • More than 8 apartments proposed in any phase
  • Both new projects and ongoing projects (that hadn't received completion certificates as of 2017)

How to Check RERA Status in Telangana

Verifying a project's RERA registration takes less than 2 minutes:

  1. Visit the official portal: rera.telangana.gov.in
  2. Click on "Registered Projects" or use the search function
  3. Search by project name, developer name, or RERA registration number
  4. Review the project details — approved plans, timelines, and financial disclosures

What you'll find on the portal:

  • Project completion timeline committed by the developer
  • Total number of apartments and those already sold
  • Layout plan and approved building permissions
  • Details of land title and encumbrances
  • Quarterly progress updates (mandatory for developers)

How RERA Protects Buyers

Before RERA, buyers had almost no recourse against delayed projects or misleading advertisements. Here's what RERA guarantees:

1. Delivery Timeline Accountability

Developers must deliver possession by the date mentioned in the RERA registration. If they miss the deadline, buyers can claim interest on the amount paid — or even a full refund with interest.

2. Carpet Area Transparency

All sales must be based on carpet area (the actual usable area within walls), not the inflated super built-up area. This prevents developers from charging you for common corridors and parking ramps.

3. No Pre-Launch Sales Without Registration

Developers cannot collect any money — not even a booking amount — before obtaining RERA registration. This ensures the project has basic approvals in place.

4. 70% Funds in Escrow

Developers must deposit 70% of buyer payments in a dedicated escrow account. This money can only be used for that specific project's construction, preventing fund diversion.

5. Structural Defect Warranty

Developers are liable for structural defects and poor workmanship for 5 years after possession. Any defects reported within this period must be rectified at the developer's cost within 30 days.

Penalties Under RERA

RERA has real teeth. Here's what developers face for non-compliance:

  • Selling without registration: Penalty up to 10% of project cost, or imprisonment up to 3 years
  • False information: Penalty up to 5% of project cost
  • Non-compliance with RERA orders: Imprisonment up to 3 years with fine
  • Agents operating without registration: ₹10,000/day penalty

How to File a Complaint

If a RERA-registered project violates its commitments, you can file a complaint online:

  1. Visit rera.telangana.gov.in and click "File a Complaint"
  2. Register yourself as a complainant
  3. Provide project details and nature of grievance
  4. Upload supporting documents (agreement, payment receipts, communication)
  5. RERA must dispose of the complaint within 60 days

Key Takeaway

RERA is the most powerful protection available to home buyers today. Always verify the RERA number before paying even ₹1 to any developer. If a project isn't registered, treat it as a red flag — no matter how attractive the price or location seems.
