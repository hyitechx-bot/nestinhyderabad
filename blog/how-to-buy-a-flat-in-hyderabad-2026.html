The Flat-Buying Journey at a Glance

Buying a flat is the biggest financial decision most people make, and doing it in the right order removes most of the stress and risk. Here is the complete step-by-step process for Hyderabad in 2026 — each step links to a detailed guide so you can go deeper wherever you need.

Step 1: Set a Realistic Budget

Start with the total cost, not the sticker price. Budget 15–20% above the base price for GST, stamp duty and registration, parking, corpus and interiors. If your ceiling is ₹1 crore, look at flats with a base price around ₹80–85 lakhs.
