3 Mistakes First-Time Home Buyers Make in Hyderabad
Don't Let These Cost You Lakhs!

Buying your first flat is exciting — but a few common oversights can turn your dream purchase into a financial nightmare. Here are the top 3 mistakes and how to avoid them.

📝 Buyer Education•July 30, 2026•5 min read

Mistake #1: Not Checking RERA Registration

This is the single biggest mistake first-time buyers make in Hyderabad. RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority) registration is mandatory for all residential projects above 500 sq. meters or more than 8 apartments. Yet many buyers sign booking forms without ever verifying the RERA number.

Why it matters:

  • An unregistered project has no legal accountability for delivery timelines
  • You cannot file complaints with RERA if something goes wrong
  • The project may not have proper approvals from HMDA/DTCP

What to do: Visit rera.telangana.gov.in and search using the project name or RERA number. If it's not listed, ask the developer why — and think twice before proceeding.

Mistake #2: Ignoring Carpet Area vs Super Built-Up Area

Most advertisements in Hyderabad quote the super built-up area (SBU), which includes common areas like lobbies, staircases, and even the swimming pool deck. The actual usable space — your carpet area — can be 30-35% less than the advertised number.

Here's a real example:

  • Advertised size: 1,800 sq. ft. (super built-up)
  • Actual carpet area: ~1,170–1,260 sq. ft.
  • You're paying for 500+ sq. ft. you'll never walk on

What to do: Always ask for the carpet area in writing. Under RERA, developers must sell based on carpet area. Compare properties using carpet area, not SBU, to get a true price-per-sq-ft comparison.

Mistake #3: Skipping Independent Legal Verification

Many buyers rely entirely on the builder's lawyer or the bank's legal check. This is risky. Bank legal verification only confirms the property is good enough for a loan — it doesn't cover all ownership disputes, litigation history, or encumbrance issues.

What can go wrong:

  • The land may have pending litigation or ownership disputes
  • Revenue records may not match the actual boundaries
  • There could be unpaid property taxes or pending dues
  • The power of attorney chain may have gaps

What to do: Hire an independent property lawyer (costs ₹5,000–₹15,000) to verify:

  • Title deed and ownership chain for 30+ years
  • Encumbrance certificate (EC) from the sub-registrar
  • HMDA/DTCP layout approval and building permission
  • No-objection certificates from relevant authorities

Quick Checklist Before You Book

  • ✅ RERA number verified on the official portal
  • ✅ Carpet area confirmed in writing
  • ✅ Independent legal verification completed
  • ✅ All approvals (HMDA, fire NOC, environment) in place
  • ✅ Builder-buyer agreement reviewed by your lawyer

Taking these steps adds maybe 1-2 weeks to your buying process — but can save you lakhs and years of stress. Your first home deserves that due diligence.
