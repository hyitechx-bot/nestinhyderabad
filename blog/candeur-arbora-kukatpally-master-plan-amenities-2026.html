Kukatpally's 20-Acre Gated Community: Master Plan & Amenities Guide 2026
Closer to Life, Closer to Each Other

Why Read the Master Plan First

Large communities look alike in brochures: pool, gym, park. What differs is how the land is used. This project, listed under the registered name Candeur Arbora (also marketed as Candeur Kukatpally), sits on 20 acres in the middle of Kukatpally's IT hub. With 75% of the land kept as open space, roughly 15 of those 20 acres are open. That is a generous ratio for a 13-tower development and it shapes the light, the walking distances and the views from your balcony.

How the Master Plan Is Organised

The plan lists 49 numbered features. Grouped by how you will actually use them, they fall into six zones:

1. Arrival and access

An entry portal, podium entry ramp, arrival plaza with a water feature, a driveway and pedestrian pathway, tower drop-offs and a separate south entry and exit. A security check post and waiting lounge sit at the gate. The point of this zone is to keep vehicles on the edge and let the interior stay walkable.

2. Pool, clubhouse and social spaces

A swimming pool with deck, the clubhouse, a juice bar, a BBQ pavilion and family pods, plus pergolas with seating and a multi-purpose lawn (the "Maidan") for events and festivals.

3. Courts and active play

A skating rink, three cricket practice strips, a multi-play court, mini soccer court, basketball court, padel court, a mini-golf putting green, a trampoline and a rock-climbing wall. Cyclists get a cycle track and a cyclers' plaza.

4. Children's zones

A kids' adventure play zone, a tot lot for toddlers, hopscotch, giant ludo and giant chess. On a visit, check which play area is closest to the tower you are considering.

5. Nature and wellness

The forest deck seating and tree court are the core of the 4.3-acre central park. Around them are a flower garden, a butterfly garden, a herb and spice garden, a reflexology path, an outdoor gym, calisthenics stations, a meditation and yoga deck, senior citizens' seating decks and a pet park. Peripheral planting wraps the boundary.

6. Culture and community

A temple in the north-east corner facing east, and a flag point. Small details like this matter to many families when they choose a community.

The 1,36,000 Sq.Ft Multi-Level Clubhouse

The clubhouse is built over several levels and is described as curated indoor space for wellness, recreation and social life. The headline features are:

  • Wellness and recreation: an indoor temperature-controlled swimming pool, an outdoor swimming pool and a VRV-enabled golf course.
  • Sports and activities: a pickleball court, a padel court, a mini scooter court and a boxing ring.
  • Family: a kids' adventure zone and a butterfly garden.

An indoor, temperature-controlled pool means you can swim all year, including through Hyderabad's hot summer. Ask on your visit for the full floor-wise clubhouse list, opening hours and how membership and maintenance are charged.

Approvals and Status

The project is registered with Telangana RERA under P02200011363 and is GHMC approved. It is currently in its pre-launch phase. To verify it yourself, open rera.telangana.gov.in, choose Registered Projects and search the registration number. Compare the sanctioned plan, unit areas and possession timeline there with your agreement.

Price
