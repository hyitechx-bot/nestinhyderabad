Registration & Stamp Duty Charges in Telangana 2026
Complete Cost Breakdown

Know exactly how much you'll pay in government charges before buying property in Telangana. No surprises at the sub-registrar office.

📝 Buyer Education•July 30, 2026•5 min read

Current Registration & Stamp Duty Rates in Telangana (2026)

When you buy any property in Telangana, the government levies three mandatory charges before the property is legally transferred to your name. These are non-negotiable and must be paid at the time of registration.

  • Stamp Duty: 7.5% of property value
  • Registration Fee: 0.5% of property value
  • Transfer Duty: 1.5% of property value
  • Total Government Charges: 9.5% of property value

The charges are calculated on the higher of two values – the agreement value (what you're paying the seller) or the government's market value (circle rate/ready reckoner rate) for that area.

Cost Example: ₹1 Crore Flat in Hyderabad

Let's calculate the exact registration cost for a flat valued at ₹1,00,00,000:

  • Stamp Duty (7.5%): ₹7,50,000
  • Registration Fee (0.5%): ₹50,000
  • Transfer Duty (1.5%): ₹1,50,000
  • Total Payable: ₹9,50,000

That's nearly ₹10 lakhs just in government charges. For a ₹60 lakh flat, you'd pay approximately ₹5.7 lakhs.

Under-Construction vs Ready-to-Move: What Changes?

The timing of your purchase affects your total tax liability significantly:

Under-Construction Property

  • GST at 5% applies on the agreement value (no input tax credit)
  • Stamp duty and registration are paid only at the time of registration (usually at possession)
  • You pay GST during construction + stamp duty at registration
  • Total government charges: 5% GST + 9.5% at registration = up to 14.5%

Ready-to-Move Property (with OC)

  • No GST applicable
  • Only stamp duty + registration + transfer duty = 9.5%
  • All charges paid at one time during registration

Documents Required for Registration

Keep these documents ready before visiting the sub-registrar office:

  • Sale deed (original + 2 copies)
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for the last 30 years
  • Aadhaar card of buyer and seller
  • PAN card of both parties
  • Passport-size photographs (2 each)
  • Property tax receipts
  • Approved building plan and permission documents
  • NOC from the housing society (if resale)
  • Power of Attorney (if applicable)
  • Two witnesses with Aadhaar cards

Registration Process – Step by Step

  1. Slot Booking: Book an appointment on the IGRS Telangana portal (registration.telangana.gov.in)
  2. Stamp Duty Payment: Pay stamp duty online via the portal using net banking, debit card, or challan
  3. Document Submission: Upload scanned copies of all documents on the portal
  4. Visit Sub-Registrar Office: Both buyer and seller appear in person with original documents and witnesses
  5. Biometric Verification: Thumb impressions and photos are captured
  6. Document Verification: Sub-registrar verifies all documents and payment receipts
  7. Registration Complete: Registered document is available for download within 3-5 working days

Online Payment Options

Telangana has fully digitized the payment process. You can pay stamp duty and registration fees through:

  • Net banking (all major banks supported)
  • NEFT/RTGS transfer
  • Debit card payment on IGRS portal
  • Challan at designated bank branches (SBI, AP Mahesh, etc.)

Pro Tip: Always pay online to have a clear digital trail. Avoid cash transactions for any government charges.

Important Things to Remember

  • Registration must be done within 4 months of executing the sale agreement
  • Late registration attracts a penalty of 2% per month (up to 8 months)
  • Women buyers may get a slight concession in some states, but Telangana currently offers no gender-based discount on stamp duty
  • NRI buyers follow the same rates – no additional charges
  • Joint registration (husband + wife) is common but doesn't reduce stamp duty in Telangana
