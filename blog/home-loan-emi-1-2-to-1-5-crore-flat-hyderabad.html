Home Loan EMI for a ₹1.2 to ₹1.5 Cr Flat
Hyderabad 2026 Guide

Planning to buy a 3 BHK in the ₹1.2 to ₹1.5 Cr range? Here is how much you may need to borrow, what the EMI could look like, and the extra costs to budget for.

💰 Finance Guide • Updated September 2026 • 7 min read

How Much Can You Borrow?

The Reserve Bank of India sets maximum loan-to-value (LTV) limits: 90% for loans up to ₹30 lakh, 80% for loans up to ₹75 lakh, and 75% for loans above ₹75 lakh. For a flat in the ₹1.2 to ₹1.5 Cr range, banks will normally fund up to 75% of the property value, and you pay the rest from your own funds. Your final limit also depends on your income, existing EMIs and credit score.

The Down Payment and Extra Costs

  • Own contribution: at least 25% of the property value if the loan is above ₹75 lakh.
  • GST: applies to under-construction flats. Confirm the current rate and whether input tax credit applies with your seller.
  • Stamp duty, transfer duty and registration fee: charged by the Telangana government on the higher of the sale price or the government value. Published sources quote different totals depending on the area and the rule version, so confirm the exact figure at your sub-registrar office or on the state registration website.
  • Maintenance deposit, corpus fund and parking: ask for these in the cost sheet.
  • Loan processing fees and legal charges: vary by bank.

Budget extra above the base price so there are no surprises at registration.

How a Construction-Linked Plan Helps

For under-construction homes, banks usually release the loan in stages linked to construction progress, so you pay interest only on the amount released. The Bachupally project in our listings follows a flexible 20:30:30:20 payment plan, which splits payments into four milestones. Ask the seller for the exact milestone schedule so you can plan your pre-EMI interest and cash flow.

Home Loan Checklist

  1. Check the project's RERA registration (Bachupally P02200010709; Kukatpally P02200011363, GHMC approved).
  2. Confirm that your bank has approved the project for loans.
  3. Compare rates, fees and prepayment rules from at least three lenders.
  4. Get a sanction letter before you pay the booking amount.
  5. Keep a buffer of 6 months of EMIs in savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much home loan can I get for a ₹1.2 crore flat?

Banks usually fund up to 75% of the property value for loans above ₹75 lakh, so up to about ₹90 lakh for a ₹1.2 crore flat, subject to your income and credit profile.

What is the EMI for a ₹1.5 crore flat?

With a 75% loan (₹1.125 crore) over 20 years, the EMI is roughly ₹97,630 at 8.5% and ₹1,04,865 at 9.5%. These are illustrative. Check current bank rates.

Is GST charged on an under-construction flat?

GST generally applies to under-construction residential flats. The rate and whether input tax credit applies depend on the project, so confirm in the cost sheet.

What are the registration charges in Hyderabad?

Stamp duty, transfer duty and registration fees are charged on the higher of the sale price or the government value. Confirm the current percentage with the sub-registrar or the Telangana registration website.

Does a 20:30:30:20 plan reduce my loan interest?

It can help, because banks release the loan in stages. You pay interest only on the amount released, so your early EMI burden is lower.
