NRI Guide to Buying a Flat in Hyderabad
Rules, Documents & Payments 2026

Living abroad and planning a home in Hyderabad? This guide explains who can buy, how to pay, what documents you need, and how to manage the purchase from overseas. Projects covered: Candeur Eternia, Bachupally and Candeur Kukatpally (Candeur Arbora) (RERA P02200011363).

🌍 NRI Guide • Updated September 2026 • 7 min read

Can an NRI Buy a Flat in Hyderabad?

Yes. Under India's foreign exchange rules, Non-Resident Indians and Overseas Citizens of India can generally buy residential and commercial property in India without prior RBI approval. Agricultural land, plantation property and farm houses are not allowed. Because rules can change, speak to a chartered accountant or lawyer before you pay any amount.

How to Pay

  • Through inward remittance from abroad via normal banking channels.
  • From your NRE, NRO or FCNR(B) account maintained with an Indian bank.
  • Cash, traveller's cheques and foreign currency notes are not accepted.
  • Keep every bank receipt, because you may need it later for tax and repatriation records.

Documents Usually Needed

  • Passport and visa or work permit (PIO or OCI card where applicable)
  • Overseas address proof and PAN card
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Income proof if you plan to take a home loan
  • A Power of Attorney, if a family member will sign in India

Managing the Purchase from Overseas

  1. Shortlist digitally: use video walkthroughs, floor plans and the RERA registration number to verify the project.
  2. Verify RERA: check the Telangana RERA portal for the project registration.
  3. Give a Power of Attorney if needed: executed at the Indian consulate or with an apostille, depending on the country.
  4. Pay through banking channels: from your NRE, NRO or FCNR(B) account, or by inward remittance.
  5. Complete the agreement and registration: either in person or through your attorney.

Home Loans for NRIs

Many Indian banks and housing finance companies offer home loans to NRIs. They usually look at your overseas income, employment history and credit record, and the loan is repaid from your NRE or NRO account. See our home loan EMI guide for illustrative numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can NRIs buy residential property in Hyderabad?

Yes. NRIs and OCIs can generally buy residential and commercial property in India without prior RBI approval, but not agricultural land, plantation property or farm houses. Confirm current rules with an advisor.

How can an NRI pay for a flat?

By inward remittance through banking channels or from an NRE, NRO or FCNR(B) account. Cash and traveller's cheques are not accepted.

Can I buy without visiting India?

Often yes, with a video walkthrough and a Power of Attorney for a trusted person in India. Execute the document as required by the Indian consulate or with an apostille.

Can NRIs get a home loan?

Yes. Many lenders offer NRI home loans based on overseas income and credit history, with repayment from an NRE or NRO account.

How do I verify the project?

Check the RERA registration on the Telangana RERA portal. The Bachupally project is registered under P02200010709 and the Kukatpally project under P02200011363.
