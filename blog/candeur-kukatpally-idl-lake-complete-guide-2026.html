Candeur Kukatpally: Complete Guide 2026
20 Acres | 13 Towers | 75% Open Space

Everything about this 20-acre gated community in Kukatpally: sizes, amenities, location, EOI booking and approvals.

Complete Guide • Updated September 2026 • ~8 min read

Introduction: A New Candeur Address in Kukatpally

Closer to life, closer to each other. Candeur Constructions, the developer behind Candeur Eternia in Bachupally, has launched a new high-rise community in Kukatpally. It is registered as Candeur Arbora and marketed as Candeur Kukatpally. It sits on 20 acres within Kukatpally's IT hub, with 13 towers, 75% open space and a 1,36,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse.

This guide covers the specifications, amenities, home sizes, location, booking process and approvals, so you can decide whether to visit. For a shorter summary, see our pre-launch overview, or for the layout in detail, the master plan and amenities guide.

Project Highlights

  • 20 acres: the total land parcel.
  • 13 towers: rising up to 25 floors.
  • 75% open space: most of the site is open and landscaped rather than built over.
  • 4.3-acre forest-themed central park: the green centre of the master plan.
  • 1,36,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse: indoor wellness, recreation and social spaces.
  • 3 BHK homes from 1,565 to 2,205 sq.ft: in three size bands.
  • Site: bounded by 30 m wide roads on two sides.

3 BHK Sizes and Layouts

The project offers 3 BHK homes only, in three bands:

  • 1,565 / 1,575 sq.ft: 2 balconies, 2 toilets and a separate utility area.
  • 1,800 / 1,830 / 1,880 / 1,905 sq.ft: 2 balconies, 3 toilets and a separate utility area.
  • 2,150 / 2,205 sq.ft: 2 balconies, 3 toilets and a separate utility area.

All homes feature large balconies, straight or L-shaped halls, separated utility areas and branded finishes. There is no 2.5 BHK in this project.

Amenities: Clubhouse and Outdoors

Clubhouse (1,36,000 sq.ft, multi-level):

  • Indoor temperature-controlled swimming pool
  • Outdoor swimming pool
  • VRV-enabled golf course
  • Pickleball court and padel court
  • Mini scooter court and a boxing ring
  • Kids' adventure zone and a butterfly garden

Across the 20-acre campus, the master plan also includes a skating rink, three cricket practice strips, a multi-play court, mini soccer and basketball courts, a trampoline, a rock-climbing wall, an outdoor gym, calisthenics stations, a mini-golf putting green, a meditation and yoga deck, a reflexology path, a pet park, a cycle track, a multi-purpose lawn, a juice bar and a BBQ pavilion.

Location and Connectivity

The community is positioned centrally within Kukatpally's IT hub: minutes from work, away from the noise.

  • Metro: about 2 km from three Metro stations (Kukatpally Metro, Y Junction, Bharat Nagar).
  • Outer Ring Road: direct access.
  • Airport: under 30 minutes to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport.
  • Malls: about 2.6 km from three malls: Manjeera Mall, Forum Sujana Mall, Ashoka One Mall and LakeShore Mall are the named options.
  • Hospitals: about 3 km from three hospitals: Apollo, Ankura, Yashoda and Rainbow Children's Hospital.
  • Schools: about 7 km from three schools: Meridian School, Euro School, Creek Planet School and DPS School.

Distances are approximate and depend on route and traffic. Kukatpally is a mature locality, so the daily infrastructure is already in place. Compare it with other areas in Kukatpally vs Bachupally.

Approvals: RERA and GHMC

The project holds Telangana RERA registration P02200011363 and is GHMC approved. RERA registration means buyer funds are held in an escrow account, the possession timeline is legally binding and buyers have a formal grievance forum. GHMC approval confirms the building plan complies with municipal rules.

You can check the registration yourself at rera.telangana.gov.in: open Registered Projects and search the number.

EOI: How Early Booking Works

The project is in its pre-launch phase. An EOI (Expression of Interest) lets you register early, lock the quoted price and get priority when choosing a tower, floor and unit. Special pricing is offered for the first 50 bookings.

Before you pay an EOI amount, get these in writing:

  • The EOI amount and whether it is refundable, and under what conditions.
  • The price per sq.ft, the floor-rise charges and the full cost sheet including GST, registration and parking.
  • The payment schedule and the RERA possession date.

Pre-launch prices start from about ₹1.2 Cr for a 3 BHK, depending on size, floor and tower.

Who This Project Suits

  • IT professionals: a home within Kukatpally's IT hub, with Metro and ORR close by.
  • Families who want space and green cover: 75% open space and a 4.3-acre park.
  • Active households: pools, golf, pickleball, padel, skating, cycling and kids' zones.
  • Upgraders: buyers moving from older Kukatpally apartments who want to stay in the same area.
