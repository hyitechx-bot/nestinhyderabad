Kukatpally Pre-Launch 2026
Sizes, Amenities & What to Know Before You Book

A 20-acre gated community with 13 towers up to 25 floors, 75% open space and a 1,36,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse. 3 BHK homes from 1,565 to 2,205 sq.ft.

Pre-Launch Guide • Updated September 2026 • ~8 min read

Closer to life, closer to each other. Candeur Kukatpally, registered as Candeur Arbora and also marketed as Candeur Kukatpally, is a new high-rise community in the middle of Kukatpally's IT hub. It is in its pre-launch phase. Here is what the project offers, how its homes are laid out and what to check before you book.

Project Overview

  • Land: 20 acres
  • Towers: 13 towers rising up to 25 floors
  • Open space: 75%
  • Central park: 4.3 acres, forest-themed
  • Clubhouse: 1,36,000 sq.ft, multi-level
  • Homes: 3 BHK, 1,565 to 2,205 sq.ft
  • Approvals: Telangana RERA P02200011363 and GHMC approved
  • Site: bounded by 30 m wide roads on two sides

With three quarters of the land kept as open space, the 13 towers sit within a large landscaped campus rather than a dense block. The central park is the heart of that plan, with forest deck seating, a tree court, a flower garden and a butterfly garden.

Location and Connectivity

The project is positioned centrally within Kukatpally's IT hub: minutes from work, away from the noise. In numbers:

  • Metro: about 2 km from three Metro stations: Kukatpally Metro, Y Junction and Bharat Nagar.
  • Outer Ring Road: direct access.
  • Airport: under 30 minutes to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport.
  • Malls: about 2.6 km from three malls. Named options are Manjeera Mall, Forum Sujana Mall, Ashoka One Mall and LakeShore Mall.
  • Hospitals: about 3 km from three hospitals. Named options are Apollo, Ankura, Yashoda and Rainbow Children's Hospital.
  • Schools: about 7 km from three schools. Named options are Meridian School, Euro School, Creek Planet School and DPS School.

Distances are approximate and depend on route and traffic. Kukatpally is a mature locality, so schools, hospitals, malls and public transport are already in place. For a wider view, see our KPHB and Kukatpally guide.

The 1,36,000 Sq.Ft Multi-Level Clubhouse

The clubhouse is described as 1,36,000 sq.ft of curated indoor wellness, recreation and social space. The amenities listed for the project are:

  • Wellness and recreation: indoor temperature-controlled swimming pool, outdoor swimming pool and a VRV-enabled golf course.
  • Sports and activities: pickleball court, padel court, mini scooter court and a boxing ring.
  • Family: kids' adventure zone and a butterfly garden.

The master plan adds many outdoor features around the clubhouse, including a skating rink, three cricket practice strips, a multi-play court, mini soccer court, basketball court, trampoline, rock-climbing wall, outdoor gym, calisthenics, a mini-golf putting green, a meditation and yoga deck, a reflexology path, a pet park, a juice bar and a BBQ pavilion.

Unit Configurations and Sizes

This is a 3 BHK-only community, with homes in three size bands:

Configuration Area (sq.ft) Layout
3 BHK 1,565 / 1,575 2 balconies + 2 toilets + utility
3 BHK 1,800 / 1,830 / 1,880 / 1,905 2 balconies + 3 toilets + utility
3 BHK 2,150 / 2,205 2 balconies + 3 toilets + utility

Across all sizes the highlights are large balconies, straight or L-shaped halls, separated utility areas and branded finishes. The main difference between the bands is the toilet count: two in the smallest, three in the larger two. If you host guests or have elders at home, the extra toilet is worth considering.

Construction, Safety and Sustainability

  • Structure: advanced RCC shear-wall construction.
  • Safety and access: comprehensive fire safety systems, 24x7 CCTV monitoring and smart access control.
  • Sustainability: integrated EV charging infrastructure.

Price and Pre-Launch Benefits

Pre-launch pricing starts from about ₹1.2 Cr for a 3 BHK, depending on size, floor and tower. Buyers at this stage typically get:

  • Early-bird pricing: the lowest rates before the formal launch. Prices usually step up as the project moves ahead.
  • Priority selection: first choice of tower, floor and size.
  • Price lock through an EOI: an expression of interest protects the quoted price until booking.

Terms change, so ask for the current offer, the payment schedule and the full cost sheet including GST, registration and parking.

Who This Project Suits

  • IT professionals: the project sits within Kukatpally's IT hub, with Metro stations about 2 km away and direct ORR access.
  • Families who want established infrastructure: schools, hospitals and malls are already operating in the area.
  • Active families: the courts, pools, cycle track and children's zones suit households that will use the amenities daily.
  • Upgraders from older Kukatpally apartments: you can move to a modern gated community without changing your children's schools or daily routine.

How to Book

  1. Enquire: contact us on WhatsApp for the price sheet and floor plans.
  2. Visit the site: see the location and review the master plan.
  3. Choose a unit: pick the tower, floor and size band with our team's help.
  4. Lock the price: pay the EOI amount if you want to hold the quoted price.
  5. Complete formalities: KYC and booking documents. We can help with home loan pre-approval.

Before you pay anything, verify the registration yourself on rera.telangana.gov.in using number P02200011363.
