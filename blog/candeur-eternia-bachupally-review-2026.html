Eternia Bachupally Review 2026
Amenities, Location & Buyer Verdict

A detailed, balanced review of one of Bachupally's most talked-about gated communities — what it offers, who it suits, and whether it's worth it.

Project Review • September 2026 • ~9 min read

Overview: What Is Eternia?

Eternia is a premium 7.7-acre gated community in Bachupally by Constructions — a developer with an established track record in Hyderabad's western corridor. The project features 4 towers rising to G+33 floors, with 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK apartments and over 68,000 sq.ft of amenities spread across two grand clubhouses. It positions itself as Bachupally's tall, amenity-rich landmark for families who want space and lifestyle without moving to the pricier Gachibowli belt.

In this review we look at the project honestly — the genuine strengths, the things to weigh, and who it's the right fit for. For the full specification and pricing details, see the Eternia project page.

Configurations & Space

Eternia's mix of 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK covers a wide band of buyers:

  • 2 BHK: Efficient for couples, small families and investors targeting the rental market.
  • 2.5 BHK: The standout format — the extra half-room works as a home office, nursery, or guest room. In a post-remote-work world, this configuration has become highly desirable.
  • 3 BHK: Generous family living with separate dining, balconies and utility spaces.

Amenities: 68,000+ Sq.Ft Across Two Clubhouses

The amenity package is one of Eternia's strongest cards. With 68,000+ sq.ft across two clubhouses, residents get full-sized facilities rather than token spaces — two swimming pools, cardio gym, preview theatre, squash and badminton courts, a restaurant, 12 guest rooms, a banquet hall, a trim trail, cycling track, two pickleball courts, kids' play areas and landscaped gardens. For a 7.7-acre project, this amenity ratio is genuinely premium and a clear differentiator from smaller Bachupally developments.

Location & Connectivity

Bachupally has transformed from a quiet suburb into one of Hyderabad's most active residential corridors. Eternia benefits from:

  • ORR access (~5 min): Quick connection to the entire city — Gachibowli, Hitech City and the airport.
  • Hitech City (~20 min): Reasonable IT-corridor commute at a lower price point than living in the corridor itself.
  • Upcoming Myron Mall (~1 min): Major retail arriving on the doorstep — a real lifestyle and property-value catalyst. See our Myron Mall analysis.
  • Schools & daily needs: Astra Lumina School is right beside the project, with Silver Oaks (1.9 km), The Creek Planet (7 mins) and Kennedy High Global (8 mins) nearby, plus Reach Super Speciality Hospital about 7 mins away.

Pros & Cons

Pros:

  • Large 68,000+ sq.ft amenity package across two clubhouses
  • Tall G+33 towers with good views and light
  • Versatile 2.5 BHK format
  • Strong ORR connectivity and upcoming Myron Mall
  • Reputed developer with delivery track record

Things to weigh:

  • Bachupally commute to the core IT corridor is longer than living in Gachibowli/Kondapur (though pricing reflects this).
  • As with any under-construction project, confirm the possession timeline and RERA status before booking.

Verdict: Who Should Buy Eternia?

Eternia is a strong fit for families and IT professionals who want a spacious, amenity-rich home in a growing corridor at better value than the Gachibowli belt — and who see upside from Bachupally's ongoing infrastructure and retail growth. If a big amenity package, a versatile 2.5 BHK, and ORR connectivity rank high on your list, Eternia deserves a shortlist spot.
