East vs West Facing Flat
Which Direction is Actually Better?

A practical comparison covering Vastu, sunlight, ventilation, and energy costs to help you choose the right flat orientation.

📝 Buyer Education•July 30, 2026•5 min read

Does Flat Direction Really Matter?

When buying a flat in Hyderabad, the direction your apartment faces affects everything — from your electricity bill to morning routines. East-facing flats are traditionally preferred in India, but west-facing flats have their own set of advantages that many buyers overlook. Let's break down the real differences.

Sunlight Patterns: Morning vs Evening

East-facing flats receive direct morning sunlight from 6 AM to 11 AM. This means bright, naturally lit living rooms in the first half of the day. Morning sun is gentle, warm, and provides a natural source of Vitamin D without excessive heat.

West-facing flats get afternoon and evening sun from 1 PM to 6 PM. This sunlight is more intense, especially during Hyderabad's long summers (March to June). However, during winter months (November to January), the afternoon warmth is actually pleasant and keeps the flat cozy.

Ventilation and Airflow

In Hyderabad, prevailing winds typically blow from the southwest during monsoon season (June–September). West-facing flats on higher floors can benefit from better cross-ventilation during these months. East-facing flats may need ceiling fans or AC to compensate during still afternoons.

However, the actual ventilation depends more on the building layout — whether you have windows on two sides (cross-ventilation), the floor level, and surrounding structures blocking airflow.

Energy Costs: The Real Impact

West-facing flats tend to have higher AC costs in summer. The harsh afternoon sun heats up walls and windows, raising indoor temperature by 3–5°C compared to east-facing units in the same building. In Hyderabad's peak summer (April–May), this can add ₹1,000–₹2,000 to your monthly electricity bill.

East-facing flats stay relatively cooler in the afternoon as the sun moves away. You'll likely need less AC time, saving on energy costs across the year. The difference is more pronounced on upper floors where heat absorption is higher.

Vastu Considerations

According to Vastu Shastra, east-facing homes are considered highly auspicious. The east direction is associated with the rising sun, new beginnings, and positive energy. The main entrance facing east is believed to bring prosperity and good health.

West-facing is not inauspicious in Vastu — it's simply associated with different energies. Vastu allows west-facing homes with certain corrections like placing the main door in the west's mid-section and avoiding a kitchen in the southwest corner. Many Vastu consultants confirm that a well-designed west-facing flat with proper internal placement is perfectly fine.

Which Floors Benefit Most from Each Direction?

East-facing + lower floors (1st–4th): Ideal combination. You get soft morning light without excessive heat, and lower floors naturally stay cooler.

East-facing + upper floors (8th+): Still good, but you may get more direct sun exposure through larger windows. Ensure good window treatments.

West-facing + lower floors: Manageable — surrounding trees and buildings often provide shade during harsh afternoon hours.

West-facing + upper floors: Most heat exposure. If choosing this combination, look for flats with double-glazed windows, external shading, or deep balconies that block direct sun entry.

The Hyderabad Climate Factor

Hyderabad has a semi-arid climate with temperatures reaching 42–45°C in peak summer. The city gets about 300 sunny days per year. This makes flat direction more impactful here than in cooler or coastal cities. The intense western sun during April–May is a genuine comfort concern, not just a Vastu preference.

However, Hyderabad also has pleasant winters where west-facing flats enjoy warm, golden evening light that east-facing flats miss entirely. If you work from home, this afternoon light can be quite uplifting during the cooler months.

The Verdict

Choose east-facing if: Vastu is important to you, you're on an upper floor, you want lower AC bills, or you're a morning person who loves natural wake-up light.

Choose west-facing if: You get a significant price advantage (often ₹50–₹100 less per sq.ft.), you have deep balconies or good shading, the flat has cross-ventilation, or you enjoy warm evening sunlight.

Ultimately, a well-designed flat with good ventilation, proper window placement, and cross-airflow matters more than direction alone. Don't reject a great flat only because it faces west.
