What Is an OTP (One Time Payment) Offer?

In an OTP offer, you pay the full value of the flat, or most of it, in one go at an early stage of the project. Because the developer receives funds upfront, it offers a lower rate per sq.ft than its standard construction-linked plan, where you pay in stages as the building rises.

  • Lower rate per sq.ft – the main reason buyers choose OTP
  • Price locked early – you are not exposed to later price revisions on your unit
  • Simpler payments – one main payment instead of many milestone demands
  • Higher upfront commitment – you pay before the project is complete, so verification matters more

Checklist Before You Pay

  • RERA registration – ask for the RERA number and verify it on rera.telangana.gov.in
  • Registered agreement of sale – under the RERA Act, a developer cannot take more than 10% of the cost as an advance without first signing and registering an agreement of sale
  • Bank payments only – pay by bank transfer to the project account, never in cash
  • Approvals and title – check building approvals and the land title documents
  • Possession date and penalties – make sure both are written into the agreement
  • Home loan – confirm your bank has approved the project and will disburse for an OTP purchase
  • Full cost sheet – get GST, registration and all charges in writing

Frequently Asked Questions – OTP Offer

What does OTP mean in real estate?

OTP stands for One Time Payment. You pay the full value of the flat, or most of it, in one go at an early stage, and in return the developer offers a lower rate per sq.ft than its standard construction-linked plan.

What is the OTP price at Sree Nivasam Infra IDL Kukatpally?

The OTP offer rate is ₹5,299 per sq.ft*. Base values are about ₹1.09 Cr for 2,050 sq.ft, ₹1.19 Cr for 2,250 sq.ft, ₹1.35 Cr for 2,550 sq.ft and ₹1.93 Cr for 3,650 sq.ft, before taxes and charges.

Is GST and registration included in the OTP rate?

No. The ₹5,299 per sq.ft rate is the base rate. GST, stamp duty, registration, parking, clubhouse, corpus and maintenance charges are extra. Ask for the developer's full cost sheet before you decide.

Can I take a home loan for an OTP purchase?

Often yes, but check with your bank first. Some banks release under-construction loans only in stages, which may not suit an upfront OTP payment. Confirm the bank has approved the project and will disburse the amount you need.

What should I check before paying under an OTP offer?

Verify the RERA registration on rera.telangana.gov.in, sign and register the agreement of sale before paying more than 10% (as required by the RERA Act), pay only by bank transfer to the project account, and check the possession date and penalty clauses in the agreement.
