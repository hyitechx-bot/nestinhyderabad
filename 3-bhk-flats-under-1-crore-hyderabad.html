Best Locations & Value Guide 2026

Is a 3 BHK Under ₹1 Crore Still Possible in Hyderabad?

Yes — but in specific corridors and under specific conditions. As Hyderabad's premium locations like Kondapur and Gachibowli have breached ₹9,000–₹12,000/sq.ft, the sub-₹1 Crore 3 BHK has shifted to the emerging western and northern corridors. These are areas with solid infrastructure fundamentals — ORR access, metro connectivity, growing commercial activity — but priced at ₹5,000–₹7,000/sq.ft because they are still maturing.

Under ₹1 Crore, you're typically looking at under-construction gated communities ranging from 1,400 to 1,860 sq.ft carpet area, with full amenity packages including pool, gym, children's play area, and 24/7 security. The trade-off is a 2–3 year wait for possession and location that may be 15–25 km from central IT hubs. For buyers who don't need immediate occupancy and are comfortable with a growing neighbourhood, this remains excellent value.

What Do You Actually Get Under ₹1 Crore?

Setting realistic expectations matters. Here's what a sub-₹1 Crore 3 BHK delivers in 2026:

  • Size: 1,400–1,860 sq.ft super built-up area, translating to approximately 950–1,250 sq.ft carpet area
  • Stage: Predominantly under-construction (ready-to-move 3 BHKs under ₹1Cr are rare in established areas)
  • Amenities: Basic to mid-range — swimming pool, gym, children's play area, security. Don't expect grand clubhouses or extensive landscaping
  • Location: Emerging corridors with improving but not fully developed retail, schools, and hospitals
  • GST: 5% GST applies on under-construction properties. Budget for this — it adds ₹4–5 Lakhs to your total cost on a ₹90L flat
  • Possession: Typically 2–3 years. Factor in rental costs during this period

3 Ways to Find a 3 BHK Under ₹1 Crore

  • Pre-launch pricing: Projects in early pre-launch phase often offer 10–15% below their eventual launch price. For a project that will launch at ₹1.1 Cr, pre-launch pricing can bring it under ₹1 Cr — but only if the developer is RERA registered.
  • Under-construction in emerging corridors: Kollur, Bachupally, and Lingampally have active under-construction inventories in the ₹80L–₹1Cr bracket. Lock in now before possession-stage pricing kicks in.
  • East Hyderabad alternatives: Uppal, Narapally, and Pocharam offer 3 BHK options at ₹70L–₹90L — the price reflects longer commute distances to western IT hubs, but proximity to Infosys Pocharam and the upcoming metro extension makes these viable.
