A 20-Acre Gated Community in Kukatpally

Candeur Kukatpally brings large-format gated community living to one of Hyderabad's most established residential areas. The 20-acre site has 13 towers rising up to 25 floors, with 75% of the land kept as open space and a 4.3-acre forest-themed central park at its heart. The tagline sums up the idea: closer to life, closer to each other.

The project is positioned centrally within Kukatpally's IT hub, about 2 km from three Metro stations and with direct access to the Outer Ring Road.

FAQs – Gated Community Flats in Kukatpally

How big is Candeur Kukatpally?
