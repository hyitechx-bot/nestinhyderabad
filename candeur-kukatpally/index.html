About the Project

Why Candeur Kukatpally?

A landmark 20-acre development by premium, offering an unmatched living experience near Kukatpally with 75% open space and a 4.3-acre central park.

Premium Living Redefined – ₹1.2 Cr onwards

Candeur Kukatpally is set to redefine luxury living in Hyderabad's western corridor. Spread across 20 acres with 75% open space and a 4.3-acre forest-themed central park, this premium gated community features 13 residential towers rising up to 25 floors, offering spacious 3 BHK apartments ranging from 1,565 to 2,205 sq.ft.

The project boasts an approx. 1,36,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse — one of the largest in Hyderabad — with world-class wellness, recreation and social spaces designed for every age group. EOI option available — special pricing for first 50 bookings only.

Quick Answers

Candeur Kukatpally – Quick Answers

Which 3 BHK sizes are available, and which have 3 toilets?

Candeur Kukatpally offers only 3 BHK, from 1,565 to 2,205 sq.ft: 1,565 / 1,575, 1,800 / 1,830 / 1,880 / 1,905, and 2,150 / 2,205 sq.ft. The larger size bands have 3 toilets.

Is there a 3 BHK of about 1,800 sq ft or 2,000 sq ft in Kukatpally?

The 1,800, 1,830, 1,880 and 1,905 sq.ft bands suit a 3 BHK of about 1,800 sq ft. There is no exact 2,000 sq.ft plan; the nearest options are 1,905 and 2,150 sq.ft.

Does it have an indoor swimming pool, padel court and pickleball court?

Yes. Amenities include an indoor temperature-controlled swimming pool, an outdoor swimming pool, a VRV golf course, a pickleball court, a padel court, a mini scooter court, a boxing ring, a kids' adventure zone and a butterfly garden, set around a 4.3-acre forest-themed central park.

How big is this gated community and how much is open space?

It is a 20-acre gated community with 13 towers up to 25 floors and 75% open space, with a 1,36,000 sq.ft multi-level clubhouse.

Which metro stations and malls are nearby?

Kukatpally Metro, Kukatpally Y Junction and Bharat Nagar metro stations are about 2 km away. Manjeera Mall, Forum Sujana Mall, Ashoka One and LakeShore are about 2.6 km away. The project has direct ORR access.
