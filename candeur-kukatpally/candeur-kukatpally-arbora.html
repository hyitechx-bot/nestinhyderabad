Why This Kukatpally IDL Lake Address Stands Out

Set across 20 acres in Hyderabad's thriving western corridor, this premium gated community is designed around open space, lake views and resort-style living. With 13 residential towers rising to G+25 floors and over 4.5 acres of landscaped Central Park, it offers the scale and planning that compact projects simply cannot match — all within minutes of Kukatpally Metro, KPHB, JNTU and the ORR.

Every home is a spacious 3 BHK, sized from roughly 1,800 to 2,207 sq.ft, planned for cross-ventilation, natural light and comfortable family living. Pre-launch pricing starts at ₹1.2 Cr onwards, with early-bird advantages reserved for the first set of bookings.
