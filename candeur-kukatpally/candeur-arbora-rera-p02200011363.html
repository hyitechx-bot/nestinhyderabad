RERA Registration – What It Means for Buyers

Candeur Kukatpally IDL Lake is registered under Telangana RERA with registration number P02200011363. RERA approval is the strongest legal protection available to a property buyer in India. Here's what it means for you:

  • Escrow Account: At least 70% of funds collected from buyers must be deposited in a designated escrow account and can only be used for construction costs of this project.
  • Legally Binding Possession Date: The developer is legally obligated to deliver possession by the registered date. Delays attract compensation at the SBI highest marginal cost lending rate.
  • No Changes Without Consent: The developer cannot make changes to the sanctioned plan, layout or specifications without written consent of at least two-thirds of buyers.
  • Transparent Disclosures: All project details — land title, approvals, floor plans, financial statements — are publicly available on the RERA portal.
  • Grievance Redressal: Buyers can file complaints directly with the Telangana RERA authority. The adjudicating officer can award compensation and penalties.
  • Home Loan Approved: All major banks process home loans for RERA registered projects. The RERA number P02200011363 enables straightforward loan sanction.

What is the difference between RERA approval and GHMC approval?

RERA approval (P02200011363) is from the real estate regulator — it governs project timelines, fund management and buyer rights. GHMC approval is from the municipal authority — it confirms the building plans, heights and setbacks comply with Hyderabad's municipal regulations. Candeur ARBORA has both.
