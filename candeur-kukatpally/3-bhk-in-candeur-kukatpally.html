3 BHK – Smaller Size Bands (1,565–1,905 Sq.Ft)

The smaller 3 BHK bands are the most popular family configuration — three well-proportioned bedrooms, a spacious living-dining area, modular kitchen with utility area, and 2–3 bathrooms.

  • Master Bedroom – King-sized with walk-in wardrobe space, ensuite bathroom and balcony access
  • Second Bedroom – Full-sized children's or guest room with wardrobe and study nook
  • Third Bedroom – Versatile room for elderly parents, guests or a home office
  • Living & Dining – Open-plan design with high ceilings and floor-to-ceiling windows
  • Kitchen – L-shaped or U-shaped with utility/washing area, chimney provision and ample storage
  • Balconies – 2–3 balconies with views of the landscaped greens

3 BHK – Larger Size Bands (2,150–2,205 Sq.Ft)

The larger 3 BHK bands offer more space for living and dining, with 2 balconies, 3 toilets and a separate utility area.

  • Grand Living Room – Expanded living space suitable for large gatherings and family time
  • Separate Dining Area – Dedicated dining zone distinct from the living room
  • Foyer/Entrance Lobby – Welcoming entry space that adds privacy to the living area
  • 3 Bedrooms – All bedrooms are full-sized with wardrobe space
  • 3 Toilets – Master ensuite, common bath and guest bath

Family-Focused Design Philosophy

  • Zoned Living – Bedrooms are clustered away from the living/kitchen zone for privacy
  • Natural Ventilation – Cross-ventilation design reduces AC dependency
  • Child Safety – Balcony railings, window grills and safe electrical points as standard
  • Senior-Friendly – Anti-skid tiles in bathrooms, grab bar provisions, wide corridors
  • Community Access – Direct lift access to clubhouse level, kids' play areas visible from towers
  • Green Views – Garden-facing options overlooking the 4.3-acre forest-themed central park available across towers

Specifications & Finishes

Frequently Asked Questions – 3 BHK

What is the size range of 3 BHK at Candeur Kukatpally?

3 BHK apartments range from 1,565 sq.ft to 2,205 sq.ft across multiple size bands, each with 2 balconies, 2 to 3 toilets and a separate utility area.

How many bathrooms are in the 3 BHK?

The 1,565 / 1,575 sq.ft band has 2 toilets. The 1,800 to 1,905 sq.ft and 2,150 / 2,205 sq.ft bands have 3 toilets. Every band has 2 balconies and a separate utility area.

Are there garden-facing 3 BHK units available?

Yes. Garden-facing units overlooking the 4.3-acre forest-themed central park are available subject to tower and floor. Contact us for available facing options.

Is there parking for 3 BHK apartments?

Yes. 3 BHK apartments come with dedicated covered parking. The larger size bands may include provision for two parking slots, subject to availability.
