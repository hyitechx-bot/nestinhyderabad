Frequently Asked Questions – Location

Which Metro stations are near Candeur Kukatpally?

Three Metro stations are about 2 km away: Kukatpally Metro, Y Junction and Bharat Nagar. They are on the Red Line; for HITEC City, change at Ameerpet to the Blue Line.

Is Candeur Kukatpally near the Outer Ring Road?

Yes. The project has direct access to the Outer Ring Road, which links to HITEC City, Gachibowli, the Financial District and the airport.

How far is the airport?

Rajiv Gandhi International Airport is under 30 minutes away.

Which malls, schools and hospitals are nearby?

Three malls are about 2.6 km away (Manjeera, Forum Sujana, Ashoka One and LakeShore are named nearby). Three hospitals are about 3 km away (Apollo, Ankura, Yashoda and Rainbow Children's). Three schools are about 7 km away (Meridian, Euro, Creek Planet and DPS).
