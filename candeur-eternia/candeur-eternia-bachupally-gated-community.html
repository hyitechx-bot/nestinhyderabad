Why This Bachupally Address Stands Out

Bachupally has quietly become one of Hyderabad's smartest value buys. It offers the quality and amenities of the western IT corridor — without the premium price tags of Gachibowli or the Financial District. This 7.7.7-acre gated community sits at the heart of that opportunity: close to ORR Exit 4, a short drive from Miyapur, KPHB and Pragathi Nagar, and roughly 20 minutes from Hitech City.

Spread across 4 towers rising to G+33 floors, the community is designed around open space, two grand clubhouses and more than 68,000 sq.ft of amenities. With 2 BHK, 2.5 BHK and 3 BHK homes ranging from 1,462 to 2,192 sq.ft, there is a layout to suit first-time buyers, growing families and work-from-home professionals alike — all from ₹1.2 Cr onwards.
