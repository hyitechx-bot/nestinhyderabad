Why This Payment Plan Saves You Money

  • Low entry barrier – just 20% to book and lock your preferred unit, floor and facing at today's price
  • Lower pre-possession interest – with a home loan, the bank disburses in stages against milestones, so you pay interest only on the amount drawn — not the full loan from day one
  • Pay as it rises – the bulk (60%) is spread across construction and structure stages, aligned with visible progress
  • Price lock advantage – booking early protects you from future price escalation as the project nears completion
  • Milestone transparency – every payment maps to a RERA-governed project milestone, so you always know what you're paying for

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 20:30:30:20 mean?

It's the split of your payment across four stages — 20% on booking, 30% during construction, 30% on structure completion and 20% on handover.

How much do I need to book a 3 BHK?

The booking stage is 20% of the unit value. For a ₹1.3 Cr 3 BHK, that's about ₹26 Lakhs (base consideration, before taxes and charges).

Does this work with a home loan?

Yes. The project is RERA registered (P02200010709) and banks disburse the loan against the same construction milestones, keeping pre-EMI interest low.

Are GST and registration included?

No. The percentages apply to the base apartment consideration. GST, registration, stamp duty, corpus and maintenance are additional — we'll give you the full cost sheet on request.

Which configurations does the plan cover?

All configurations — 2 BHK, 2.5 BHK and 3 BHK (1,462–2,192 sq.ft). 3 BHK starts from around ₹1.3 Cr.
