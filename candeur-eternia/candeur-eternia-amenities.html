Overview Candeur Eternia Amenities

Candeur Eternia sets a new benchmark for amenity-rich living in Bachupally, Hyderabad. With over 68,000 sq.ft of dedicated amenity space spread across two grand clubhouses, residents enjoy a resort-like lifestyle every single day. Whether you're a fitness enthusiast, a family with young children, a senior citizen, or someone who loves to entertain there's something here for everyone.

The amenities are thoughtfully divided across two grand clubhouses Clubhouse 1 (G+5) focused on fitness, sports and wellness, and Clubhouse 2 (G+4) dedicated to hospitality, celebrations and guest accommodation. The Double Height Clubhouse Lobby and Double Height Tower Lobby set the tone from the moment you arrive.

FAQs Candeur Eternia Amenities

How many amenities does Candeur Eternia have?

Clubhouse 1 (68,000+ sq.ft) has 19 amenities, Clubhouse 2 has 5, and the outdoor masterplan lists 40 legend items covering sports, play areas, lawns and landscaped spaces.

Does Candeur Eternia have a swimming pool?

Yes. Clubhouse 1 has two swimming pools, and the outdoor masterplan also includes a swimming pool and deck.

Is there a gym in Candeur Eternia?

Yes. Clubhouse 1 has a cardio gym and a gym, and the outdoor area has an outdoor gym and trim trail.

Can I host events at Candeur Eternia?

Yes. Clubhouse 2 has a banquet hall, multi-purpose hall, restaurant/dining hall, 12 guest rooms and an open terrace, suited to parties and family gatherings.
