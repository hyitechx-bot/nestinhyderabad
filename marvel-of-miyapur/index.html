About the Project

Why Marvel of Miyapur 3.0?

Every home is a corner home — premium living with unmatched ventilation and natural light in Miyapur's finest gated community.

Corner Homes with 30ft Gap

Marvel of Miyapur 3.0 redefines apartment living — every home is a corner home with a 30ft gap between facing flats, ensuring excellent cross-ventilation and abundant natural light. Large balconies offer panoramic views of the landscaped community.

Located opposite D-Mart Miyapur, this ~9 acre gated community is just minutes from Miyapur Metro station and offers quick access to Hitech City via ORR. Multiple towers rising 10 floors offer spacious 3 BHK homes ranging from 1,860 to 2,560 sq.ft.
