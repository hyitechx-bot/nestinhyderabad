Why IT Professionals Choose Manikonda in 2026

Manikonda is Hyderabad's established IT-professional hub, sitting right between Gachibowli (5 minutes) and the Outer Ring Road (5 minutes). The area has matured over the past decade with excellent social infrastructure — schools, hospitals, shopping, restaurants — making it one of the most livable neighborhoods in Hyderabad's western corridor.

Unlike newer areas that are still developing basic infrastructure, Manikonda offers the comfort of an established neighborhood with immediate access to Hyderabad's top employment hubs. The area also benefits from strong rental demand, with 2 BHK rentals averaging ₹25,000–₹35,000/month and 3 BHK commanding ₹35,000–₹55,000/month.

Manikonda Apartment Prices in 2026

Manikonda offers a range of options from affordable to premium:

  • 2 BHK (1,000–1,350 sq.ft): ₹80 Lakhs – ₹1.3 Crore
  • 3 BHK (1,400–1,900 sq.ft): ₹1.2 Crore – ₹2 Crore
  • 3 BHK Premium (2,000+ sq.ft): ₹1.8 Crore – ₹2.8 Crore
  • Average rate: ₹6,500 – ₹9,000 per sq.ft

Manikonda has seen steady appreciation of 12-15% annually. The proximity to IT hubs ensures consistent demand from both end-users and investors.
