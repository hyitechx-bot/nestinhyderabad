A Premium Gated Community in the Heart of Bachupally

Bachupally has quietly become one of Hyderabad's smartest value corridors — delivering the quality, scale and amenities of the western IT belt without the Gachibowli price tag. This 7.7-acre gated community sits right in that sweet spot: minutes from ORR Exit 4, about 10 minutes from Miyapur X Roads, and roughly 20 minutes from Hitech City.

Planned across four residential towers rising to G+33, the community is built around open space, two clubhouses and a 40-item outdoor masterplan. It is developed by a well-established Hyderabad builder and carries full Telangana RERA registration (P02200010709).

Frequently Asked Questions
