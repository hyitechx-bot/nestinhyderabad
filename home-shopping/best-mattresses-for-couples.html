Firmness and Comfort

  • Side sleepers usually prefer medium-soft to medium.
  • Back and stomach sleepers usually prefer medium-firm to firm.
  • If you and your partner differ, medium is a common compromise.
  • A thickness of about 6–8 inches is common.

For Hyderabad's Heat

Look for breathable covers, cooling gel or latex layers, or pocket springs for airflow, especially in rooms without AC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which mattress type is best for couples?

Memory foam and pocket spring mattresses both reduce partner disturbance. Pocket springs sleep cooler; memory foam absorbs movement best.

Queen or king size for a couple?

A queen (about 60 × 78 inches) suits most couples. Choose a king (about 72 × 78 inches) if you want more space and the room allows it.

How firm should a couple's mattress be?

If your sleeping styles differ, a medium mattress is a common compromise.

How do I keep a mattress cool in summer?

Choose breathable covers, cooling gel or latex layers, or pocket springs for better airflow.
