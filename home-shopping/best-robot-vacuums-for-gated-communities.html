Navigation: The Most Important Choice

NavigationHow it worksBest for
LiDAR / laserMaps the home accurately and cleans in neat linesLarger 2.5BHK and 3BHK flats, multiple rooms
Camera / gyroscopeSimpler mappingSmaller flats or open layouts

Features to Look For

  • Mop function: vacuum-and-mop models suit vitrified tiles.
  • Suction power: higher suction picks up fine dust better.
  • Battery run time: enough to clean your whole flat in one go.
  • App control: schedules, no-go zones and room-by-room cleaning.
  • Self-empty station: less frequent emptying, at a higher cost.
  • Threshold climbing: check it can cross door sills and rugs.

Tips for Flats

  • Clear loose cables and small items before a run.
  • Set no-go zones around pet bowls and balconies.
  • Run it daily while you are at work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a robot vacuum work on tile floors?

Yes. Robot vacuums work well on the vitrified tile floors most new flats have, and mop models clean them too.

Which navigation is best for a 3BHK?

LiDAR navigation maps larger homes accurately and cleans room by room.

Does a robot vacuum replace manual cleaning?

It handles daily dust; you will still need occasional deep cleaning in corners and under heavy furniture.
