Frequently Asked Questions

Which fire extinguisher is best for a home?

An ABC-type extinguisher covers most household fires. Keep it near the kitchen exit and check its expiry date.

Can I install a balcony safety net in a high-rise flat?

Usually yes, but check your society's rules first, and make sure nothing blocks windows or openings meant as emergency exits.

Where should I place a smoke detector?

In the hallway near the bedrooms. Test the battery every month.
