Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add bathroom shelves without drilling tiles?

Use adhesive, no-drill shelves and holders. They avoid cracking tiles or damaging the waterproofing behind them.

Is a robot vacuum good for Indian homes?

It works well on vitrified tile floors, which most new flats have. Mop-and-vacuum models handle daily dust.

How do I remove hard-water stains?

Use a descaling cleaner made for bathroom fittings and tiles, and clean regularly so stains don't build up.
