Set Up From Day One

New bathrooms usually have tiled walls up to the false ceiling. Adhesive, no-drill accessories avoid cracking tiles or damaging the waterproofing. If your society uses borewell water, hard water leaves white stains, so a shower filter and regular descaling help. Most new flats have vitrified tile floors, which suit cordless vacuums and spin mops.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fix bathroom accessories without drilling tiles?

Use adhesive, no-drill accessory sets. They avoid cracking tiles or damaging the waterproofing behind them.

What size geyser do I need?

A 15 to 25 litre storage geyser suits most bathrooms. Check that the bathroom has a geyser point.

Do I need a fire extinguisher at home?

A small ABC-type extinguisher near the kitchen exit is a sensible precaution. See our home safety guide for more.
