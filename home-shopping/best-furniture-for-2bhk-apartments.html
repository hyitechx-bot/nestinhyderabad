Measure First

Floor plans show room sizes, but furniture must also fit through the lift and the main door. Measure the lift, door widths and each room's usable wall length before ordering large pieces. Leave about 2.5 to 3 ft of walking space around beds and sofas.

Living Room

A 3-seater sofa is usually about 6 to 7 ft long. In a compact living room, a 3-seater with two side chairs often works better than a large L-shaped sofa.

Frequently Asked Questions

What furniture do I need for a 2BHK flat?

Start with a sofa, TV unit, dining set, two beds with mattresses, wardrobes and a shoe rack. Add a study table if you work from home.

What is the size of a queen bed in India?

A queen bed is usually about 60 × 78 inches, and a king bed about 72 × 78 inches.

Should I buy furniture before moving in?

Measure the rooms, doors and lift first. Order large items once you know the final layout, and schedule delivery after the flat is cleaned.
