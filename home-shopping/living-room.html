Frequently Asked Questions

What size sofa fits a flat living room?

A 3-seater sofa of about 6 to 7 ft suits most compact living rooms. Choose an L-shaped sofa only if the room has a long free wall.

How far should the sofa be from the TV?

A common rule of thumb is about 1.5 to 2.5 times the TV's screen size.

Are BLDC fans worth it?

BLDC fans use less electricity than older ceiling fans, which helps in Hyderabad where fans run for much of the year.
