Plan the Layout

Frequently Asked Questions

What size sofa fits a 2BHK living room?

A 3-seater sofa of about 6 to 7 ft suits most 2BHK living rooms. Choose an L-shaped sofa only if the room has a long free wall.

What dining table size suits a compact flat?

A 4-seater table suits most 2BHK dining areas. An extendable table gives extra seats when guests visit.

How do I save space in a small living room?

Use wall-mounted TV units and shelves, a storage coffee table and a folding study table that tucks away.
