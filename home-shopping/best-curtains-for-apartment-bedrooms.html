For West-Facing Rooms

In Hyderabad, west-facing rooms get strong afternoon sun. Thermal or blackout curtains with a lining keep these rooms cooler and help your AC work less.

How to Measure

  • Width: total curtain width of about 1.5 to 2 times the window width gives a full look.
  • Rod position: about 4–6 inches above the window frame and 6–10 inches wider on each side.
  • Length: floor-length looks taller and blocks more light; sill-length suits windows above furniture.

Heading Styles

  • Eyelet: easy to slide on a rod; modern look.
  • Pleated: neat folds; works with tracks.
  • Tracks suit ceiling mounting and wide windows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are blackout curtains worth it?

Yes for bedrooms. They block light for better sleep and reduce heat from the afternoon sun in west-facing rooms.

How wide should curtains be?

The total curtain width should be about 1.5 to 2 times the window width.

Where should the curtain rod go?

About 4 to 6 inches above the window frame and 6 to 10 inches wider on each side.
