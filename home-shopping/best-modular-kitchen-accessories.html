Plan Before You Buy

In many new Hyderabad projects, the kitchen is handed over with plumbing for the sink and electrical points for the hob, chimney, RO and refrigerator, but no counters or cabinets. If you are getting a modular kitchen made, decide on these accessories at the design stage, because baskets and pull-outs depend on cabinet widths.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accessories should I include in a modular kitchen?

Most families start with tandem baskets, a cutlery organiser, a corner unit for L-shaped kitchens, a bottle pull-out, a spice rack and an under-sink organiser.

Should I buy kitchen accessories before or after the cabinets?

Plan them before the cabinets are made. Baskets and pull-outs depend on cabinet widths, so share your list with your carpenter or modular kitchen vendor.

Do new flats in Hyderabad come with a modular kitchen?

Many do not. Projects often provide sink plumbing and appliance points only. Check your builder's specifications.
