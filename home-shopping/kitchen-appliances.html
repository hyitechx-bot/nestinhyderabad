Before You Buy

Check where your kitchen's electrical points are. New flats usually provide points for the hob, chimney, RO, refrigerator, microwave and mixer, and some larger homes also have a dishwasher provision in the utility area. Place countertop appliances near a socket so you don't depend on extension boards. For the refrigerator, chimney, hob and RO purifier, see our appliances guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kitchen appliances do I need for a new flat?

Most families start with a mixer grinder, microwave or OTG, electric kettle and induction cooktop as a backup, then add a wet grinder, air fryer or dishwasher based on how they cook.

Is a dishwasher useful for Indian cooking?

Yes, if you choose a model with a heavy-soil or Indian cooking cycle. Check that your flat has a dishwasher water and power point first.

What wattage of mixer grinder should I buy?

A 750W mixer grinder with three jars handles most Indian wet and dry grinding.
