Before You Buy

New flats usually come with electrical and plumbing points for the hob, chimney, RO, refrigerator, microwave and mixer, but not the appliances themselves. Note where each point is before you order, and keep countertop appliances near a socket. For the refrigerator, washing machine and AC, see our appliances guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kitchen appliances should I buy first for a new flat?

Most families start with a mixer grinder, water purifier, electric kettle and induction cooktop, then add an air fryer, OTG or chimney based on how they cook.

Do new flats in Hyderabad come with a kitchen chimney?

Usually not. Most projects provide an electrical point and exhaust provision for the chimney, and you buy and install it yourself.

Which water purifier is right for my flat?

Test your water TDS first. Low-TDS treated water may only need UV or UF, while high-TDS borewell or tanker water usually needs RO.
