A Suggested Order

  1. Inspect the flat and set up utilities (see the moving-in checklist).
  2. Plan and install the modular kitchen and appliances.
  3. Fit curtains, lights and ACs.
  4. Bring in furniture.
  5. Add smart devices and decor.
