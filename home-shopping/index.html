A Suggested Order

  1. Inspect the flat and set up utilities (see the moving-in checklist).
  2. Plan the kitchen and buy core appliances.
  3. Fit curtains, lights, fans and ACs.
  4. Bring in furniture and mattresses.
  5. Add smart devices, cleaning tools and decor.
