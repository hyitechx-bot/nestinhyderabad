1. At Handover: Inspect the Flat

  • Check walls and ceilings for cracks, damp patches and paint finish.
  • Run every tap and flush, and look for leaks under sinks.
  • Test every switch, socket and the main distribution board.
  • Open and close all doors and windows; check locks and fly-mesh provisions.
  • Check tiles for hollow sounds, cracks or uneven joints.
  • Note every issue in writing and share it with the builder before you sign the handover.

2. Documents to Collect

  • Registered sale deed and possession letter.
  • Occupancy certificate details, if issued.
  • Warranty cards for fittings and any installed equipment.
  • Paid receipts for all payments, including maintenance and corpus.

3. Utilities and Society

  • Transfer the electricity meter to your name with the local power distribution company.
  • Set up gas: piped gas if your society has it, or a cylinder connection.
  • Book an internet connection; ask your society which providers serve the building.
  • Register with the residents' association and get access cards, parking stickers and move-in permission.
  • Check the society's rules for moving hours and lift booking.

4. Before Moving Day

  • Get a deep cleaning done.
  • Consider pest control before furniture arrives.
  • Install curtains, lights and ACs first, then bring furniture.
  • Book packers and movers early, especially at month-end.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I check at flat handover?

Inspect walls, ceilings, plumbing, electrical points, doors, windows and tiles. List every issue in writing and share it with the builder before signing the handover.

What should I buy first for a new flat?

Start with cleaning supplies, a tool kit, extension boards, a first aid kit, bulbs, bedding and a basic kitchen set.

What utilities do I need to set up when moving in?

Transfer the electricity meter to your name, arrange gas (piped or cylinder), book internet and register with your residents' association.
