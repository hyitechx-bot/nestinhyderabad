Build Quality

  • Frame: solid wood such as sheesham lasts longer than engineered wood.
  • Foam: higher-density seat foam holds its shape longer.
  • Legs: raised legs make cleaning underneath easier.

Fabrics for Hyderabad Weather

  • Fabric upholstery stays cooler than leatherette in summer.
  • Removable, washable covers suit homes with kids and pets.
  • Darker or textured fabrics hide stains better.

Measure Before You Order

Measure your lift, main door and any corridor turns. Larger sofas often come in parts; confirm this with the seller.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sofa size suits a 2BHK flat?

A 3-seater sofa of about 6 to 7 ft suits most 2BHK living rooms.

Fabric or leatherette for Hyderabad?

Fabric stays cooler in warm weather. Choose washable covers if you have kids or pets.

Is a sofa cum bed a good idea?

Yes, if your flat has no dedicated guest room. Check that the mechanism is easy to open and close.
