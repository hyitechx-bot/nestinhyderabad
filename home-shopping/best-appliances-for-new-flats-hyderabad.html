Start With What Your Flat Already Has

Before you buy, check your builder's specifications and handover kit. Many new flats in Hyderabad come with provisions only: electrical and plumbing points for the AC, geyser, RO, chimney, washing machine and dishwasher, but not the appliances themselves. Note where each point is, and measure the space before you order.

Air Conditioner

Hyderabad summers are long and hot, so most families buy ACs first. Choose by room size and exposure:

  • Up to about 120 sq.ft: 1 ton is usually enough.
  • About 120 to 180 sq.ft: 1.5 ton is the common choice for most bedrooms.
  • Above 180 sq.ft or a large living room: 2 ton.
  • Top floor or west-facing room: go one size up, as these rooms heat up more.

Refrigerator

  • 1–2 people: about 190–250 litres (single door or compact double door).
  • 3–4 people: about 250–350 litres double door.
  • 5 or more people: 350 litres or more, or a side-by-side model if space allows.

Measure the kitchen niche, including space to open the doors fully and a few inches behind for ventilation.

Washing Machine

  • 1–2 people: about 6–7 kg.
  • 3–4 people: about 7–8 kg.
  • 5 or more people: 8 kg or more.

Front-load machines are gentler on clothes and use less water. Top-load machines are easier to load and usually cost less. If your building uses borewell water, hard water can leave scale, so look for a machine with a hard-water or descaling setting.

Water Purifier, Geyser and Kitchen Appliances

Water quality varies across Hyderabad. If your society uses treated municipal water, a UV or UV+UF purifier may be enough. If it relies on borewell water with high TDS, an RO purifier is the usual choice. A TDS meter costs little and tells you which one you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AC tonnage is right for a Hyderabad bedroom?

For most bedrooms between about 120 and 180 sq.ft, a 1.5 ton AC is the common choice. Go one size up for top-floor or west-facing rooms.

Do new flats come with appliances?

Usually not. Most new flats provide electrical and plumbing points for the AC, geyser, RO, chimney and washing machine, but you buy the appliances yourself. Check your builder's specifications.

Do I need an RO purifier in Hyderabad?

It depends on your water. Test the TDS with a meter. Low-TDS treated water may only need UV or UF, while high-TDS borewell water usually needs RO.

What size washing machine should I buy for a family of four?

A 7 to 8 kg machine suits most families of three to four people.
