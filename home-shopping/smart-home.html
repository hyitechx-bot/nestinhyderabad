Check Your Society's Rules First

Most gated communities already have CCTV, access control and intercoms. Before installing a smart lock, doorbell or camera, check your residents' association rules on door changes and camera placement, and point cameras only at your own entrance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install a smart lock in an apartment?

Usually yes. Check your door type and thickness, and confirm with your residents' association whether there are rules about door changes.

Is a robot vacuum worth it for a flat?

It works well on the vitrified tile floors most new flats have and keeps daily dust under control, especially in larger 3BHK homes.

Do I need a mesh Wi-Fi router?

In larger flats with RCC walls, a mesh system helps reach every room. Smaller flats may manage with one good router.
