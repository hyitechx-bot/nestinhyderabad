Check Your Society's Rules First

Most gated communities already have CCTV, access control and intercoms. Before installing a smart lock or doorbell, check with your residents' association about door modifications and camera placement. Point cameras only at your own entrance, not at a neighbour's door.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install a smart lock in an apartment?

Usually yes, but check your door type and thickness, and confirm with your residents' association if there are rules about door modifications.

Do I need mesh Wi-Fi in a flat?

In larger flats, especially those over 2,000 sq.ft with RCC walls, a mesh system helps reach every room. Smaller flats may manage with one good router.

Is it okay to install a camera outside my flat door?

Check your society's rules first, and point the camera only at your own entrance to respect neighbours' privacy.

Can I control my old AC with my phone?

Yes. A smart IR remote can control most ACs and TVs that use an infrared remote.
