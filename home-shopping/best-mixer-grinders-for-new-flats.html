How Much Power Do You Need?

WattageBest for
About 500WLight use: chutneys and juices
About 750WMost families: wet and dry grinding, batter, masalas
About 1000W or moreHeavy daily grinding and larger families

Jars

  • Large wet jar for batter and gravies.
  • Dry jar for masalas and spices.
  • Chutney jar for small quantities.
  • A juicer jar is a useful extra.

Other Things to Check

  • Overload protection, so the motor does not burn out.
  • Multiple speeds and a pulse option.
  • Stainless steel jars and blades.
  • Motor warranty and service network near you.
  • Noise level, especially in open kitchens.

Frequently Asked Questions

What wattage of mixer grinder is best?

A 750W mixer grinder handles most Indian wet and dry grinding.

How many jars do I need?

Three jars cover most needs: a large wet jar, a dry jar and a small chutney jar.

Should I buy a wet grinder too?

If you make idli and dosa batter often, a separate wet grinder gives a smoother batter.
