Frequently Asked Questions

How can I decorate a new flat on a budget?

Start with lighting, curtains and a rug. These change how a room feels the most for the least money. Add wall decor and plants over time.

Which curtains are best for west-facing rooms?

Blackout or thermal curtains block the strong afternoon sun and help keep the room cooler.

Which plants grow well in Hyderabad apartments?

Snake plants, money plants and areca palms are hardy, handle heat well and need little care.
