Start With Sleep

A good mattress and pillows matter more than any other bedroom purchase. Then control light and heat: west-facing bedrooms in Hyderabad get strong afternoon sun, so blackout curtains and an efficient ceiling fan make a real difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size mattress do couples need?

Most couples choose a queen (about 60 × 78 inches) or king (about 72 × 78 inches) mattress. Match it to your bed frame.

Which bedsheets suit Hyderabad weather?

Cotton bedsheets are breathable and stay cooler in warm weather.

Are BLDC ceiling fans worth it?

BLDC fans use less electricity than older fans, which adds up in Hyderabad where fans run most of the year.
