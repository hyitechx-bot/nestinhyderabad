Beds and Mattresses

  • Queen bed: about 60 × 78 inches, suits most master bedrooms.
  • King bed: about 72 × 78 inches, needs a larger room.
  • Single bed: about 36 × 78 inches, for kids or guest rooms.

Buy the mattress to match the bed size. Memory foam suits side sleepers, while firmer mattresses suit back and stomach sleepers. Check the trial period and warranty.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size bed fits a master bedroom?

A queen bed of about 60 × 78 inches suits most master bedrooms. A king bed of about 72 × 78 inches needs a larger room with walking space on both sides.

Which bedsheets are best for Hyderabad weather?

Cotton bedsheets are breathable and stay cooler in warm weather.

How do I choose a mattress?

Choose firmness by how you sleep: softer or memory foam for side sleepers, firmer for back and stomach sleepers. Check the trial period and warranty.
