Flats Near Hitech City, Hyderabad
Best Apartments for IT Professionals – 2026

A practical guide to buying apartments near Hitech City. Compare locations, prices, commute times, and metro connectivity — so you can pick the right neighbourhood without overpaying.

📍 Buyer's Guide • Updated July 2026

Hitech City is Hyderabad's largest IT employment hub, home to Microsoft, Amazon, Google, TCS, Infosys, and hundreds of mid-size tech companies. If you work here, your daily commute is the single biggest quality-of-life factor when choosing a flat. This guide breaks down every residential area within a 15-minute drive of Hitech City — with real prices, distances, and trade-offs.

Best Locations Near Hitech City

Six residential zones offer practical access to Hitech City. Each has a distinct price-to-proximity equation:

Kondapur – 3 Minutes

The closest residential area to Hitech City. Kondapur is mature, well-connected, and commands premium pricing. You get walkable access to IT campuses, excellent schools (CHIREC, Oakridge), and premium hospitals. The trade-off is cost — expect ₹9,000–₹10,500/sq.ft for gated community apartments. Ideal if commute elimination is your top priority and budget isn't a constraint.

Madhapur – 5 Minutes

Madhapur blurs the line between commercial and residential. Living here means you're essentially inside the IT corridor. Restaurants, cafes, and nightlife are at your doorstep. Prices sit at ₹8,500–₹10,000/sq.ft. Best suited for young professionals who value urban energy over quiet, family-oriented layouts.

Gachibowli – 8 Minutes

Home to the Financial District and ISB campus, Gachibowli offers wider roads, newer developments, and slightly lower density than Kondapur. Prices range from ₹7,500–₹9,500/sq.ft. Good pick for those splitting their commute between Hitech City and Financial District offices.

Kukatpally – 10 Minutes

Kukatpally offers the best value-for-money within a 10-minute radius. Established social infrastructure, KPHB metro station, affordable grocery and retail options, and prices at ₹6,000–₹8,000/sq.ft. The commute via ORR or the internal road through Hitech City takes 10–12 minutes in normal traffic. Excellent for families wanting space without burning the budget.

Manikonda – 12 Minutes

South of Hitech City, Manikonda has seen rapid development over the past 5 years. Prices are competitive at ₹5,500–₹7,500/sq.ft, and the area offers newer constructions with modern amenities. The downside: road infrastructure is still catching up in some pockets, and peak-hour traffic towards Hitech City can stretch the commute to 20 minutes.

Miyapur – 15 Minutes

Miyapur is the budget-friendly pick with metro connectivity. At ₹5,000–₹6,500/sq.ft, you get significantly more space for your money. The metro line connects directly to Hitech City in about 20 minutes (no traffic dependency). Ideal for buyers who want a larger home and don't mind a slightly longer commute — especially if metro-based.

Why IT Professionals Prefer These Areas

Reduced Commute = More Living Hours

A 10-minute commute instead of a 45-minute one gives you back over an hour daily — that's 250+ hours per year. For dual-income IT couples, proximity to Hitech City isn't a luxury; it's a practical decision that impacts work-life balance, especially with hybrid schedules requiring 3–4 office days per week.

Strong Rental Yields

Flats near Hitech City command rental yields of 3–4% — significantly higher than Hyderabad's average of 2–2.5%. A 2 BHK in Kondapur fetches ₹25,000–₹35,000/month rent; in Kukatpally, ₹18,000–₹25,000. Even if you relocate later, your property stays income-generating due to the constant demand from IT workforce.

Social Infrastructure That's Already Built

Unlike emerging areas where you wait years for schools, hospitals, and retail, these neighbourhoods have everything operational today. International schools, multi-specialty hospitals, malls, sports facilities, co-working spaces — all within a 10-minute drive. You move in and start living immediately.

Metro Connectivity

The Hyderabad Metro Blue Line is a game-changer for flats near Hitech City. Here's how each location connects:

  • Miyapur: Direct metro access (Miyapur is the terminal station). Ride to Hitech City in ~20 minutes. Best metro-connected option for budget buyers.
  • Kukatpally (KPHB): KPHB Colony metro station provides direct connectivity. 12–15 minutes to Hitech City station. No auto/cab dependency.
  • Hitech City / Madhapur: Raidurg metro station serves this belt. Useful for connecting eastward to Ameerpet, Secunderabad.
  • Kondapur: No direct metro station, but Raidurg station is accessible within 8–10 minutes by auto. The upcoming metro Phase 2 extensions may improve this.
  • Gachibowli: Currently no metro station. Depends on road-based commute. Future Phase 2 may cover this area.
  • Manikonda: No metro access. Entirely road-dependent. Plan for 15–20 minutes during peak hours.

Bottom line: If you want a metro-backed commute without traffic unpredictability, Miyapur and Kukatpally are your strongest options. If you prefer driving proximity, Kondapur and Madhapur win.
