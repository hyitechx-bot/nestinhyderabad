2 BHK Flats in Bachupally: What You Get

Bachupally has become one of north-west Hyderabad's most searched residential areas because it combines quick Outer Ring Road access with the Miyapur metro corridor. A 2 BHK in a gated community here suits first-time buyers, young families and professionals who work around Hitec City or Gachibowli.

Is a 2 BHK the Right Size for You?

  • Choose a 2 BHK if you are a couple or a small family and want the lowest entry price in a gated community.
  • Consider a 2.5 BHK (1,590 and 1,619 sq.ft) if you want a study or home office.
  • Consider a 3 BHK (1,711 to 2,192 sq.ft) if you have a growing family or want guest space.
