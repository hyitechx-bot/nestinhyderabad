What Drives 3 BHK Pricing in Kukatpally

  • Project type & amenities: Large gated communities with big clubhouses command more, and hold value better, than bare standalone towers.
  • Floor rise: Higher floors usually carry a premium for views and light.
  • Facing & views: East-facing, park-facing and lake-view units sit at the top of the range.
  • Construction stage: Pre-launch pricing is lowest and steps up with each milestone.
  • Metro proximity: The closer to Kukatpally Metro, the stronger the rental and resale demand.
