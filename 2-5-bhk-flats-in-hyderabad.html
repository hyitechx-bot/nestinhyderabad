What Is a 2.5 BHK Flat?

A 2.5 BHK is a two-bedroom home with an additional half room. Buyers usually use that extra room as a study, a work-from-home corner, a nursery or a guest space. It sits between a 2 BHK and a 3 BHK in both size and budget, which is why it suits couples, small families and people who work from home.
