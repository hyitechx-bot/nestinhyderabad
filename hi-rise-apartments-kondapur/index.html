10-Acre Hi-Rise Gated Community in Kondapur

This premium hi-rise gated community spans 10 acres in the heart of Kondapur, with 8 blocks rising to G+49 floors. It offers spacious 2, 3 & 4 BHK homes from 1,475 to 3,375 sq.ft — in both East and West facing orientations. Every flat is designed with ample ventilation for natural lighting and air.

Positioned very near the University of Hyderabad and close to Chandanagar MMTS Station, it is walkable to supermarkets, shopping malls, restaurants, schools, hospitals and much more — an excellent investment opportunity in one of Hyderabad's most established western corridors.
