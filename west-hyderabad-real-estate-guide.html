Why West Hyderabad Leads the Market

West Hyderabad is the engine of the city's real estate market. It's home to the IT corridor — Hitech City, Gachibowli and the Financial District — where the bulk of the city's high-income employment sits. That concentration of jobs drives everything: housing demand, rental strength, and steady price appreciation. Combined with metro reach in the north-west (Kukatpally/KPHB) and the ORR threading the whole belt, it's the most connected and most in-demand part of the city.

This guide maps the key areas so you can match the right micro-market to your budget, commute and goals.
